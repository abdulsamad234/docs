Not every reward comes from a sale. You can also reward affiliates for hitting a target, winning a contest, reaching a milestone, or creating content — and you can hand out a one-off bonus whenever you like. These non-sale rewards land in the same [Rewards list](/help-center/manage/rewards/overview) as commissions, so everything an affiliate earns is in one place. This page explains how to spot them and where they come from.

## How they appear in your Rewards list

A reward that isn't tied to a sale doesn't have a customer, so instead of a name its row shows a purple **gift icon**. The label tells you which kind it is:

- **Bonus** — a bonus you gave or an affiliate earned. Underneath, the row shows the reason for the bonus, or **No reason provided** if none was set.
- **Content rewards** — a reward for content an affiliate submitted.

Open one and its detail panel names it the same way, with the reason shown as a **Bonus Reason** so you have the context behind the payment.

## Filter to just bonuses and content rewards

When you want to see only non-sale rewards, use the **Type** filter above the list. Alongside **Sign Up** and **Purchase**, it has **Bonus** and **Content Rewards** — pick one to narrow the list to just those. This is handy for checking what you've paid out beyond regular commissions, or reviewing a run of content rewards together.

## Where bonuses come from

A reward labelled **Bonus** can be created a few ways:

- **A bonus you give by hand** — award an affiliate a one-off bonus from their detail page. See [Give a bonus](/help-center/manage/affiliates/give-a-bonus).
- **Performance bonuses** — [rewards that trigger](/help-center/gamify/performance-bonuses/overview) when an affiliate hits a target you set, like a number of sales or a revenue goal.
- **Milestones** — [rewards for reaching a milestone](/help-center/gamify/milestones/overview) in your program.
- **Contests** — [prizes for the winners](/help-center/gamify/contests/overview) of a contest you run.

Each of these drops a bonus into the Rewards list automatically when it's earned, with a reason that tells you which one it was.

## Where content rewards come from

A **Content rewards** entry comes from your [content reward campaigns](/help-center/gamify/content-rewards/overview) — where you pay affiliates for posts, videos, or other content that meets the criteria you set. When a submission is approved and qualifies, its reward appears in the list like any other.

## Approving and paying them

Bonuses and content rewards are rewards like any other, so they follow the same path. They carry a status, can sit **On Hold** waiting for your approval, and are collected into payouts once they're accepted — exactly as commissions are. Everything in [Approve or decline a reward](/help-center/manage/rewards/approve-or-decline) and the [Payouts overview](/help-center/manage/payouts/overview) applies to them too.
