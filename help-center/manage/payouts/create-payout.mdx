Referly can create payout batches for you on a schedule, but you don't have to wait for it. **Create Payout** gathers your affiliates' approved, unpaid rewards into new batches whenever you want, for example at the end of a campaign or when an affiliate asks to be paid early. You choose how old the rewards must be and how much each affiliate needs to have earned, and you can see exactly who's included before anything is created.

## Before you start

Only rewards that are ready to be paid can go into a payout:

- The reward is **Accepted**. Rewards that are on hold, declined, or refunded are left out. See [Approve or decline a reward](/help-center/manage/rewards/approve-or-decline).
- The reward hasn't been paid and isn't already in a payout. Rewards from a payout that failed can be included again.
- For cash rewards, the affiliate has set up their payout details (and their invoice details, if you require them). Coupons and credits don't need payout details.

## Open the Create payout dialog

Go to **Payouts** in the left sidebar and select **Create Payout** at the top right, next to **Manage Payout Settings**. The **Create payout** dialog opens.

If there's nothing to pay, you'll see **No unpaid rewards to pay out** instead.

![The Create payout dialog with the Rewards, Affiliates and Total payout tiles, the payout methods row, the date slider, the Set a start date switch and the minimum per affiliate slider](/images/help-center/manage/create-payout-dialog.png)

## Check what's included

The three tiles at the top update as you change the options below, so you always see what the payout will cover:

- **Rewards**: how many rewards will be paid.
- **Affiliates**: how many affiliates will be paid. Select the link under the number (for example **View 3 affiliates**) to open **Affiliates in this payout**, a list of every affiliate with their number of rewards and amount.
- **Total payout**: the total cash amount.

Under the tiles, **Payout methods that will be created** shows an icon for each payout method the new batches will use. Hover an icon to see its name.

## Choose which rewards to include

The first slider decides how old a reward must be to be included. Its label reads **Rewards earned earlier than** a number of days ago, with the matching date. Drag it to the right, toward **Today**, to include more recent rewards, or to the left, toward the earliest unpaid reward, to include only older ones.

The slider starts at your [holding period](/help-center/manage/payouts/schedule#set-the-holding-period), so by default only rewards that have cleared it are included. Moving it closer to today lets you pay rewards that are still inside the holding period, so think about refunds before you do.

To leave out older rewards too, turn on **Set a start date** ("Only include rewards earned after a certain date"). A second handle appears on the slider and the label changes to **Rewards earned between** two numbers of days ago, with both dates shown.

## Set a minimum per affiliate

The **Minimum payout per affiliate** slider sets how much an affiliate must have earned to be included. Affiliates whose unpaid rewards total less than this are left out of this payout, and their rewards stay unpaid for a later one.

The slider starts at your program's [minimum payout amount](/help-center/manage/payouts/minimum-amount). Changing it here only affects this payout, not your settings. Like the program minimum, it only applies to cash rewards, so coupons and credits are included whatever their value.

## Fix affiliates who were left out

If some affiliates have unpaid cash rewards but haven't set up their payout details, an amber message tells you how much was excluded and for how many affiliates. Select **Click here to fix it** to open **Excluded from this payout**, a list of those affiliates and their amounts.

![The Create payout dialog with the amber message that an affiliate was excluded because they have not set up their payout details, and the Click here to fix it link](/images/help-center/manage/create-payout-warning.png)

Hover the warning icon next to a name to see what's missing: either the affiliate hasn't set up their payout details yet, or they're missing invoice details you require. To fill them in yourself:

1. Select the pencil button (**Edit payout details**) next to the affiliate.
2. Add the missing details in **Edit Payout Details**. If something is missing or wasn't accepted, the field is highlighted with the problem underneath.
3. Select **Save Changes**.

The numbers in the **Create payout** dialog update once the details are saved. You can also ask the affiliate to complete their details in their portal and create the payout later.

![The Excluded from this payout list showing an affiliate with a warning icon, their unpaid amount and the pencil button to edit their payout details](/images/help-center/manage/create-payout-excluded.png)

## Create the payout

When the numbers look right, select **Create payout for** the number of rewards shown. Referly confirms how many batches it created and how many rewards they cover.

Here's what happens next:

- Referly creates one batch for each payout method and reward type. They show as **Manually Created** in the **Type** column.
- Referly Payouts batches start as **Waiting for payment**. All other batches start as **Ready**.
- If you've turned on automatic payouts for [Referly Payouts](/help-center/integrations/referly-payouts#step-4-decide-whether-payouts-run-automatically) or [PayPal](/help-center/integrations/paypal#turn-on-automatic-paypal-payouts), those batches are paid automatically.
- Each affiliate in the payout gets a notification in their portal that a payout was created.

Otherwise, open each new batch from the list to review and pay it. See [Pay a batch](/help-center/manage/payouts/batches#pay-a-batch).

## Pay specific rewards instead

If you'd rather pick individual rewards, go to **Rewards** in the left sidebar, tick the rewards you want, and select **Create Payout** in the bar that appears. Review the **Create Payout Batch** summary and select **Confirm**.

This route ignores your minimum payout amount and other payout criteria. Rewards on hold, rewards already in a payout in progress, and rewards for affiliates whose payout details aren't complete are still left out. See [Rewards overview](/help-center/manage/rewards/overview#act-on-a-reward).
