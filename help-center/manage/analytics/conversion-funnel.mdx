The **conversion funnel** shows the journey a visitor takes from clicking an affiliate's link all the way to buying, and where along the way people fall out. It sits at the bottom of the [Program Performance](/help-center/manage/analytics/performance) tab, and like the rest of that tab it reflects the [time period and filters](/help-center/manage/analytics/date-ranges) you've chosen.

## The three steps

The funnel has three stages, shown as bars that narrow as fewer people make it through:

1. **Clicks** — everyone who clicked an affiliate link.
2. **Referrals** — the visitors who became tracked referrals (the middle step; you can change this — see below).
3. **Sales** — the referrals who went on to buy.

In the top-right corner, the **conversion rate** tells you what share of all those clicks ended in a sale — your single headline number for how efficiently traffic turns into revenue.

## See the detail behind each step

Hover over any step to open its details. For each one you'll see:

- **The conversion rate from the previous step** — for example, how many of your clicks became referrals.
- **How many dropped off** — the number of people lost between that step and the one before it.
- **Value per action** — the average revenue behind each click, referral, or sale at that step.
- **Top affiliates and countries** — who and where is driving that step.

This makes it easy to find the weak point. A big drop between clicks and referrals points to a traffic-quality or landing-page problem; a big drop between referrals and sales points to something in your checkout or offer.

## Choose the middle step

Select the gear icon in the top-right of the funnel card to open its settings. The **Middle funnel step** toggle lets you choose what the middle stage counts:

- **Referrals** — visitors who became tracked referrals in the period. This is the default.
- **Carts** — unique add-to-cart events tied to affiliate clicks, so you can see how many shoppers added a product before buying.

The **Carts** view only shows numbers if add-to-cart tracking is set up for your store. This setting applies to your whole program, so changing it here changes the funnel for everyone on your team.

![funnel-settings](https://qnthgdfusotvjgaxpfie.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/public-assets/docs/uploads/1784730419769-425624-SCR-20260722-phbw.png)

## Choose what the sales step counts

The same settings popover has a **Last step: sales** toggle that changes which sales the final step includes:

- **All** — every sale in the period.
- **New** — only each referred customer's first-ever purchase.
- **Repeat** — only the sales that came after a customer's first purchase, such as renewals.

Switching to **New** shows how well your affiliates convert fresh customers, while **Repeat** shows the ongoing value of customers they referred earlier. Unlike the middle-step choice, this toggle only changes your own view.
