A store in Atlas is one website with one connection: a Shopify shop, a WordPress site with the Referly plugin, or any site that carries the tracking script. Each store has its own pixels, filters, settings and reports, and belongs to a business. The setup wizard walks you through the four steps in order, saves as you go, and can be reopened any time from the **All stores** page.

If you already connected the same site to your affiliate program, Atlas can reuse that connection. Nothing needs to be installed twice.

## Before you start

- Know which platform the site runs on. The wizard detects it from the address, and you can correct it.
- Have access to the platform: the Shopify admin, the WordPress admin, or wherever your site's head code is edited.
- Have a plan or a trial. You can finish the whole wizard without one, but the store only starts sending events once a plan is active. The setup checklist reminds you.
- Check your plan's store limit: Starter includes 1 store, Growth 3, Scale 10. At the limit the wizard asks you to upgrade before adding another.

## Open the Add a store wizard

There are three ways in, and they all open the same wizard:

- During onboarding, click **Connect your store** on the last step.
- On the **All stores** page (left sidebar, **Store**, **All stores**), click **Add store** in the top right of the business card.
- In the business switcher at the top of the sidebar, click **Add store** under the business.

![All stores page with the Add store button highlighted](/images/atlas/setup/setup--add-a-store-button.png)

The wizard is a large dialog with the four steps listed on the left: **Your website**, then **Connect Shopify**, **Connect WooCommerce** or **Tracking script** depending on the platform, then **Connect your data** (optional, custom sites only) and **Ad platforms**. A **Need help?** card at the bottom books an integration call with Referly.

## Step 1: Your website

Enter the site's address in **Website** (for example `mystore.com`). Atlas suggests the **Store name** and checks the site for its platform: "Checking your site…", then "Detected Shopify. Pick another if that is wrong."

![Add a store wizard, step 1 Your website, with the website and store name fields and the platform tiles](/images/atlas/setup/setup--step-1-your-website.png)

Pick the **Platform** tile that matches your site:

| Tile | What it sets up |
| --- | --- |
| **Shopify** | The Referly Shopify app, which installs the pixel and sends orders. |
| **WooCommerce** | The Referly WordPress plugin, which adds the script and sends orders. |
| **Ecwid** | The Referly app on Ecwid plus the tracking script. |
| **Wix**, **Squarespace**, **Webflow** | The tracking script, with instructions for that builder. |
| **Custom code / other** | The tracking script, pasted by you or through Google Tag Manager. |

Below the tiles, **Or use a site from your affiliate program** lists sites you already connected for your affiliate program. Click **Use** next to one and Atlas reuses that connection: nothing to reinstall. Click **Continue**.

Every variant ends with **Verify the connection**: Atlas looks at your site for the script or app embed and tells you whether it found it. Once this step is done, the store counts as connected.

## Step 3: Connect your data

This step appears for custom sites only (Shopify and WooCommerce already send their orders). Pick the tool that records your sign ups, trials, subscriptions or sales: Stripe, Paddle, Chargebee, Creem, Polar, Ecwid, Sharetribe, Zapier, Make or the REST API. Atlas turns what they report into conversion events for your ad platforms. Each store has one data integration. The step is optional: click **Skip for now** if the tracking script already sends everything. See [Connect your data](/help-center/atlas/setup/connect-your-data).

![Wizard step 3 Connect your data with the integration cards](/images/atlas/setup/setup-connect-your-data--tiles.png)

## Step 4: Ad platforms

Turn on the platforms you advertise on and add a pixel for each: Meta Ads, Google Ads, TikTok Ads, Snapchat Ads, Pinterest Ads, Microsoft Ads, X Ads, Taboola, Reddit Ads and Klaviyo. Each switch opens the **Add your tracking pixel** dialog, which is the same one you find later under **Pixels and destinations**. The optional box below connects ad accounts for the Ad center.

![Wizard step 4 Connect your ad platforms with a switch per platform](/images/atlas/setup/setup--step-4-ad-platforms.png)

Without a plan the step reads "Choose a plan to start sending events" with a **Choose a plan** button; your setup is saved either way. Click **Finish**. The toast reads "Store is live. Tracking is on." You can add pixels later: "No pixel yet. You can finish now and add pixels later under Pixels and destinations."

## What happens after you finish

A store goes live on its own as soon as the tracking step is done and your plan is active. It shows as **Awaiting data** until the first order arrives, then **Running**. Without a plan it waits, and moves on the moment you choose one. Every status is explained under [Store statuses](/help-center/atlas/statuses#store-statuses).

First events usually show in your ads managers within 15 to 30 minutes. Full coverage takes 24 to 48 hours, because Atlas needs new visits to link to new orders.

To reopen the wizard later, open **All stores**, click the three dots on the store row and choose **Setup wizard** (or **Finish setup** while the store is still a draft). To change the platform of a connected store, open Store settings and click **Change** next to **Platform**.

## The setup checklist

While anything is left to do, the top bar shows a **Complete your setup** pill. It opens a five-step list for the business: **Choose a plan** (or **Start your free trial**), **Add your store**, **Add a destination**, **Connect an ad account** and **Receive your first order** (or **Receive your first conversion** for SaaS and lead businesses). Each step opens the right page. **Hide checklist** hides it for the business for good; every step stays available from the sidebar.
