The **Campaigns** page of the Ad center lists **Campaigns, ad sets and ads with the revenue your store tracked beside their spend.** Every row carries the same metrics as the dashboard cards, plus two ratings that tell you at a glance whether a row is hitting your targets and which way it is moving.

![Campaigns page with the level tabs, Performance bands and metric columns](/images/atlas/ad-center/ad-center-campaigns--page.png)

The **Channels** and **Ad accounts** selectors, the date range and **Refresh** work exactly as on the [dashboard](/help-center/atlas/ad-center/dashboard#choose-channels-and-ad-accounts). With nothing ticked, the page asks you to **Select at least one ad account in Channels or Ad accounts**; with nothing synced yet, it shows **No campaigns yet** until the first sync finishes.

## Campaigns, ad sets and ads

The **Level** tabs above the table switch between **Campaigns**, **Ad sets** and **Ads**. Each row shows the name, its id, a platform icon and a status pill (hover it for **Active on Meta** and similar). To drill down, select a name: a campaign opens its ad sets, and an ad set opens its ads. On the deeper levels a line under the name shows where the row belongs, such as **Campaign: Summer Sale** or **Ad set: Lookalike 1%**.

![Campaigns table at the Ad sets level with one row per ad set](/images/atlas/ad-center/ad-center-campaigns--ad-sets-level.png)

With **All stores** selected, a **Store** column tells you which store each row's revenue was matched in.

## Columns

Hover a column header for its definition; the formulas are collected on the [Metrics](/help-center/atlas/ad-center/metrics) page. Select a header to sort by it. The columns available are:

| Group | Columns |
| --- | --- |
| Ratings | **Performance**, **vs. prior range** |
| Identity | **Name**, **Store** (All stores only), **Platform** |
| Spend and return | **Spend**, **Revenue**, **AOV**, **ROAS**, **AER**, **CPA**, **Purchases** |
| Platform's own count | **Conversions** |
| Funnel | **ATC**, **Cost per ATC**, **IC**, **Page Views** |
| Traffic | **Clicks**, **Impressions**, **CTR**, **CPC**, **CPM**, **Frequency** |
| Audience (Meta) | **New audience**, **Engaged audience**, **Existing customers** |

Your column choice is saved separately for the Campaigns, Ad sets and Ads levels.

To change the columns, click the **Customize columns** icon at the right end of the table header, tick or untick metrics, and drag to reorder them.

## Performance targets and period comparison

The two rating columns are driven by settings you reach from the table's options menu (the **Table options** button at the top right of the table).

![Table options menu with Performance targets, Period comparison settings, Show disabled and the export formats](/images/atlas/ad-center/ad-center-campaigns--table-options.png)

### Performance targets

**Performance targets** rates every row on one metric against your targets, and fills the **Performance** column with **High**, **Medium** or **Low**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick the metric">
    Choose the **Metric** the rating is based on. ROAS is the default; CPA, CTR and CPC are the other common choices.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the thresholds">
    Under **Threshold ranges**, type the value **High** starts from and the value **Low** sits below. **Medium** is everything in between and is shown as read-only text, for example **1.5x to 3x**. High has to be at least Low, or the dialog says so.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply">
    Select **Apply**. The dialog confirms with **Saved for this store.** Turn on **Apply settings for all stores** before applying to use the same targets everywhere; it overrides the settings you set per store, and the confirmation reads **Saved for every store.** **Reset to default** restores the defaults below.
  </Step>
</Steps>

![Performance targets settings with the metric set to CPA and the threshold ranges](/images/atlas/ad-center/ad-center-campaigns--performance-targets.png)

The defaults, before you change anything:

| Metric | High | Medium | Low |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ROAS | 3x and above | 1.5x to 3x | below 1.5x |
| CPA | 25 and below | 25 to 50 | above 50 |
| CTR | 1.5% and above | 0.8% to 1.5% | below 0.8% |
| CPC | 0.75 and below | 0.75 to 1.5 | above 1.5 |

Currency amounts are in the store currency.

### Period comparison

**Period comparison settings** rates how each row moved against the previous range of the same length, and fills the **vs. prior range** column with **Increased**, **Stable** or **Decreased**. Pick the metric, then the change above which a row counts as **Increased** and below which it counts as **Decreased**; the default is ROAS with a 10% band either way. The same **Apply settings for all stores** switch and **Reset to default** button are there.

## Filter, search and Show disabled

The search box above the table narrows the rows by name. **Filter** opens a menu with:

- **Campaign** and **Ad set**: tick one or more by name. Long lists show the first matches with **Type to narrow.**
- **Platform**: Meta, Google Ads or TikTok.
- **Status**: **Active**, **In review**, **Issue**, **Paused** or **Removed**. What each one means is on the [Statuses](/help-center/atlas/statuses#ad-account-and-campaign-statuses) page.
- A **Min** and **Max** range for any metric, for example Spend above 100 or ROAS below 1. The menu refuses a range where **Min must not be above Max.**

![Filter menu of the Campaigns table with Campaign, Platform, Status and the metric filters](/images/atlas/ad-center/ad-center-campaigns--filter.png)

When filters hide everything, the page says **No campaigns match** and offers to clear the search or the filters.

**Show disabled** lives in the table options menu and is on by default. Turn it off to hide paused and removed campaigns, ad sets and ads; the page then reads **Paused and removed campaigns are hidden.** when nothing active is left. Your choice is saved.

## Totals and export

The last row of the table is the total. It reads **Total · this page (25 of 112)** when it sums the rows on screen, or **Total · all 112 campaigns** when it sums every row that matches your selection and filters; switch between the two on the row itself.

**Export** in the table options menu downloads the table as **CSV** (every row as a spreadsheet), **JSON for AI** or **Markdown for AI**. The export follows the level, filters and date range on screen. Atlas confirms with **Export ready**.

## Unmapped and Unattributed rows

Two rows appear at the bottom of the table when the Ad center could not tie an order to a synced campaign. They carry revenue but no spend, so you can see what is missing from your ROAS.

| Row | What it holds | How to fix it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Unmapped (Meta)**, or another platform | Paid orders whose campaign could not be matched by id or name. The click came from that platform, but it carried no ids and its names matched nothing Atlas synced. | Paste the platform's tracking template into its ads manager so every click carries campaign, ad set and ad ids. See [UTM tagging](/help-center/atlas/reports/utm-tagging). |
| **Unattributed** | Orders with no paid click inside the lookback window: no click id and no paid visit in the 7 days before the order. | Nothing to fix if the order came from email, organic or affiliates. If you expected an ad click, check that your ads land on a page with the Atlas tracker and carry the template. |

Hover either row's name for its description. The **Mapping health** card on the [Ad accounts](/help-center/atlas/ad-center/connect-ad-accounts#mapping-health-and-tracking-templates) page shows how many of your orders were matched by id, by name, platform only, or not at all.
