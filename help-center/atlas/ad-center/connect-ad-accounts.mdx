The Ad center in Referly Atlas puts the spend from your ad accounts beside the orders your store tracked, so ROAS, CPA and the rest come from real revenue instead of the numbers an ad platform reports about itself. To get there, you connect the ad accounts that run ads for a store. Atlas reads them, never changes them: the page says it plainly, **Read-only access. We never change your campaigns.**

This guide walks through connecting each platform, picking the accounts to sync, what Atlas imports, and how to read and act on the health card of every connected account.

![Ad accounts page with the Connect a platform cards and the Connected accounts](/images/atlas/ad-center/connect-ad-accounts--page.png)

## Before you start

You need an active Atlas plan or trial. Every plan includes the Ad center; what changes is how many ad accounts you can connect:

| Plan | Ad accounts |
| --- | --- |
| Starter | 2 |
| Growth | 6 |
| Scale | 20 |
| Enterprise | Unlimited |

The count covers every store owned by your account. The same ad account connected to several stores counts once, and reconnecting an account that stopped working never uses another slot. The page shows where you stand, for example **2 of 6 ad accounts used on your plan.** See [Billing](/help-center/atlas/settings/billing) to change plan.

You also need a login with access to the ad account on the platform side: for Meta, a personal Facebook profile that can see the ad account in Meta Business Suite; for Google Ads, a Google account that can open the Google Ads account, manager accounts included, so one login can bring in every account it manages. And you need a store to attach the accounts to, because the store's orders are what the spend is matched against. If you connect while **All stores** is selected, the connect button first asks **For which store?** and lists the stores of the business.

Open the **Ad accounts** page under **Ad center** in the left sidebar. You can also start from step 4 of the Add a store wizard, **Connect your ad platforms**, which has the same Meta, Google Ads and TikTok buttons under **Connect ad accounts for Ad center**. The top of the page shows one **Connect a platform** card per platform; the **Connected accounts** section below lists what you have so far.

## Connect Meta

The Meta card covers **Facebook and Instagram ads. Read-only access to spend and ads.**

Meta is currently reviewing the Referly app. Until that review completes, Meta only lets people who are registered as testers of the app connect an ad account, so connecting Meta starts with a short request. Here is how it looks today.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Ask to be added as a tester">
    Select **Connect Meta**. The **Connect Meta** dialog explains that the app is under review and asks which Facebook profile to invite: the one that has access to your ad account in Meta Business Suite. Paste its address into **Facebook profile link** (open your profile on Facebook and copy the address bar; a page or business link will not work, it has to be a person), optionally add **Name on the profile**, and select **Request access**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for the invitation">
    The dialog switches to **Request received** and tells you that Atlas will invite that profile as a tester within one business day. The Meta card shows **Tester invite pending** in the meantime. Watch for the invitation on Facebook: it appears as a notification and is listed at developers.facebook.com/settings/developer/requests/.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Accept the invitation">
    Once Atlas has sent the invitation, the dialog reads **You have been invited** and the card shows **Invitation sent**. Sign in to Facebook with that profile, open developers.facebook.com/settings/developer/requests/ and accept the Referly Atlas invitation. It can also show up as a Facebook notification.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect">
    Come back to Atlas and select **Connect Meta** in the dialog. Facebook opens: choose the Facebook account that has access to your ad account and approve the read-only access. Meta sends you back to Atlas and the account picker opens, described in [Choose ad accounts to sync](#choose-ad-accounts-to-sync).
  </Step>
</Steps>

![Connect Meta dialog asking for the Facebook profile to invite as a tester](/images/atlas/ad-center/connect-ad-accounts--connect-meta-dialog.png)

Picked the wrong profile? Email support@referly.so with the right link and the request is swapped. Once Meta approves the app, the tester step disappears and **Connect Meta** opens Facebook straight away. After that, Atlas keeps its Meta access fresh on its own; you only connect again when Meta stops accepting the login, which the account card flags as **Reconnect**.

## Connect Google Ads

The Google Ads card covers **Search, Shopping, Performance Max and YouTube. Read-only.** Google Ads connects directly: there is no tester step.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start the connection">
    Select **Connect Google Ads**. If **All stores** is selected, pick the store under **For which store?** first. Once a Google login is already connected, the button reads **Add accounts** instead.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in and approve">
    Google opens. Sign in with the Google account that can open your Google Ads account and approve the access Atlas asks for. If that account is a manager account, every account it manages shows up in the next step.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the accounts">
    Google sends you back to Atlas and the account picker opens. Tick the accounts that run ads for this store and import them.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If Atlas reports **Google did not grant offline access. Remove Referly Atlas from your Google account permissions, then connect again.**, open the third-party access settings of your Google account, remove Referly Atlas there, and go through **Connect Google Ads** once more.

## Connect TikTok

The TikTok card covers **TikTok ads and Spark Ads. Read-only access to reports.** Today it carries a **Coming soon** pill: TikTok has not yet approved the Referly app, and the card says it is waiting for that approval. Check back on this page; the connect button appears as soon as the platform opens.

![Connect a platform cards with the TikTok card marked Coming soon](/images/atlas/ad-center/connect-ad-accounts--coming-soon.png)

Until then, TikTok conversions still reach TikTok through your [TikTok pixel](/help-center/atlas/pixels/tiktok), and TikTok campaigns still appear in [Reporting](/help-center/atlas/reports/reporting) when your ads carry UTMs. Spend and the Ad center metrics need the ad account.

## Choose ad accounts to sync

After you approve access on the platform, Atlas opens the **Choose ad accounts to sync** picker and tells you what it found: **We found 4 ad accounts for this Meta login. Atlas imports 90 days of history for each one you pick.**

Each row shows the account name, its id, its currency and a status pill. Use the search box (**Search by name, id or currency**) when the login sees many accounts.

| Pill | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Active** | The account is running normally. |
| **Unsettled** | The platform has paused delivery until an outstanding balance is paid. Atlas can still import its history. |
| **In review** | The platform is reviewing this account. Ads may not run until it is approved. |
| **Closed** or **Disabled** | The account is closed or disabled on the platform. |
| **Already connected** | This account is already in this store. |
| **Reconnect** | Already in this store behind a login that stopped working. Picking it switches it to the new login. |
| **Also in** a store | Connected to another store of your business. Adding it here does not use another ad account on your plan. |

The meter at the bottom repeats your plan position (**3 of 6 ad accounts used on your plan**, or **Unlimited ad accounts on your plan**). If you tick more than you have left, the picker asks you to uncheck some or move to a bigger plan. Select **Import 2 accounts** (the number follows your selection). Atlas confirms with **Importing history for 2 ad accounts**, or **Added 2 ad accounts with the history your other store already synced** when another store of the business had them already.

The list is kept for 30 minutes. After that the picker shows **This list expired**; select **Connect Meta again** (or the platform you were connecting) to fetch it afresh. **No ad accounts found** means the login you used sees no ad accounts: check that you picked the right Business when logging in, then select **Try another login**.

## What syncs and how often

Connecting an account starts an import that runs in the background. The account card shows its progress step by step: **Accounts**, **Campaigns and ads**, **90 days of spend**, and for Meta **Audience**, with a line such as **Importing history · 34 of 90 days**.

| When | What Atlas does |
| --- | --- |
| On connect | Imports 90 days of history: campaigns, ad sets and ads with their names and status, daily spend, and the platform's own numbers (impressions, clicks, reach, video views and reported conversions), plus the creative behind each ad. |
| Every hour | Picks up new spend and any late corrections for today and the two days before. |
| Every Sunday | Re-syncs the last 28 days so late platform adjustments land. |
| Meta only | Reads the audience and age and gender breakdowns used by the Creative center. This step is skipped while the account's API usage is above 50%. |

Spend arrives in the ad account's currency and is converted to the store currency with that day's rate. Revenue and orders never come from the platform: they come from your store. See [How revenue is matched to ads](/help-center/atlas/ad-center/dashboard#how-revenue-is-matched-to-ads).

When a sync fails, Atlas retries on its own with a growing wait, from 15 minutes up to 24 hours between tries. After five failures in a row the card reads **Sync error**. When the platform stops accepting the login, the card reads **Reconnect** and Atlas sends one email, **Reconnect Meta to keep Ad center syncing** (with the platform's name in the subject).

## Account health

Every connected account has a card under **Connected accounts** with its platform, name, a state pill, and **Synced 12 min ago · next sync in 48 min** (or **Next sync within the hour**). Below that: **History** (**90 days imported**, **Importing 90 days**, **Import failed, retrying** or **Not imported yet**), **Campaigns**, **Currency**, and **API usage** once the platform's rate limit is half used.

![Connected ad account cards showing Paused and Up to date states](/images/atlas/ad-center/connect-ad-accounts--health-cards.png)

| Pill | What is happening | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Up to date** | The last sync succeeded. | Nothing. |
| **Queued** or **Syncing** | A sync is running now; the progress block shows the step. | Wait; the card updates on its own. |
| **Import pending** | The 90-day import starts within the hour. | Wait. |
| **Retrying** | The last sync failed. Atlas retries within the next 10 minutes, or at the time shown. | Wait. If it keeps retrying, hover the pill for the platform's message. |
| **Waiting on limits** | The platform asked Atlas to slow down. Sync resumes on its own at the time shown. | Wait. |
| **Sync error** | Several syncs in a row failed. Atlas keeps retrying. | Hover the pill for the platform's message, then **Re-sync**. |
| **Paused** | Sync is paused. Nothing new is imported. | **Resume** to import new spend. |
| **Reconnect** | The platform stopped accepting this login. | Select **Reconnect**. |

The full list of Ad center states is on the [Statuses](/help-center/atlas/statuses#ad-account-and-campaign-statuses) page.

![Connected ad account cards in the Reconnect state with the platform's error message](/images/atlas/ad-center/connect-ad-accounts--reconnect.png)

### Mapping health and tracking templates

Below the accounts, the **Mapping health** card tells you how many paid clicks carry the ids the Ad center needs to credit the right campaign, ad set and ad: **82% of 1,240 paid clicks in the last 30 days carry campaign, ad set and ad ids.** The number is green at 80% and above, amber from 40%, and red below. A line per platform follows, then **Orders: 56 matched by id, 9 by name, 3 platform only, 12 with no paid click.** With no paid clicks yet, the card asks you to add the tracking templates so every click carries its ids.

![Mapping health card showing the share of paid clicks that carry ids](/images/atlas/reports/utm-tagging--mapping-health.png)

The **Tracking templates** card gives you one template per platform with a **Copy** button, where to paste it, and an **Example landing URL**. Paste each one into its ads manager and your clicks start carrying campaign, ad set and ad ids. [UTM tagging](/help-center/atlas/reports/utm-tagging) explains each template and where it goes.

![Tracking templates card with the Meta, Google Ads and TikTok tabs](/images/atlas/reports/utm-tagging--tracking-templates.png)

## Re-sync, pause, reconnect, remove

Each account card has its own controls.

- **Re-sync** imports the last 28 days again. Atlas confirms with **Re-sync queued. You can watch it below.** and the button reads **Syncing** until it is done. Use it after you fix something on the platform side, or when a card sits on **Sync error**.
- **Pause** stops the hourly sync for this account; the card shows **Paused** and what was imported so far stays in the Ad center. **Resume** turns it back on.
- **Reconnect** shows when the platform stopped accepting the login. It takes you through the platform's sign-in again, and picking the same account switches it to the new login without using another slot.
- The **More actions** menu holds the two destructive options:
  - **Remove ad account** opens **Remove Harbor Ads?** (your account's name). It removes this ad account and its synced spend from Atlas. If it is the only account from that login, the login is disconnected too; otherwise the other accounts on the login stay connected. Your orders stay. You can add it again any time.
  - **Disconnect Meta login** (or the platform in question) opens **Disconnect this Meta login?**: it removes all the ad accounts that came from this login, with their synced spend. Your orders stay. You can connect again any time. Confirm with **Disconnect 3 accounts**.

Both options delete the synced spend from Atlas. Pause the account instead if you want to keep the history while stopping new imports.
