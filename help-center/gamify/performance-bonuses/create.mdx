A performance bonus pays affiliates a fixed cash reward when they hit a goal you set. This page walks through building one. For how they work overall, see the [performance bonuses overview](/help-center/gamify/performance-bonuses/overview).

## Create a performance bonus

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Performance Bonuses">
    Select **Performance Bonuses** in the left sidebar, then choose **Create** at the top right. (If you don't have any yet, use the **Create Performance Bonus** button in the middle of the page.)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name it">
    In the **Details** section, give the bonus a **Name** your affiliates will understand, like Revenue Milestone Bonus. You can add an optional **Description** explaining the offer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the criteria">
    In the **Criteria** section, build the goal as a sentence — *when an affiliate's activity reaches a target, pay a bonus*. Choose the activity, the target, and the reward (covered just below).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose who can earn it">
    In the **Affiliates** section, pick the audience: **All affiliates**, **Specific affiliates**, or **Affiliate groups**. Only affiliates in your choice can earn this bonus.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a schedule (optional)">
    Still in **Details**, turn on **Start date** and **End date** if you want the bonus to run for a set period. Leave them off for a bonus with no time limit.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Style it (optional)">
    On the **Style** tab, give the bonus an icon and colour, or upload an image, so it stands out in your affiliates' portal.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish">
    Select **Publish**. The bonus goes live and starts watching for affiliates who qualify. Not ready to go live yet? Select **Save draft** next to it instead. See [Save a draft](#save-a-draft).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Setting the criteria

The criteria section is where you define the goal. It has four parts:

### What counts toward the goal

Choose the **activity** you're rewarding:

- **Referrals** — the number of customers the affiliate refers.
- **Conversions** — the number of purchases those referred customers make.
- **Revenue** — the total revenue the affiliate generates.

### Which activity counts

Choose the **scope**:

- **New** — counts only activity from the bonus's start date onward (or from when you create it, if there's no start date). Use this for a fresh challenge.
- **Lifetime** — counts everything the affiliate has done since they joined your program. Use this to reward total achievement.

### The target and the reward

Enter the **target** the activity has to reach, then the **bonus amount** — the fixed cash reward, in your program's currency, the affiliate earns for reaching it.

<Note>
  If some affiliates already meet the goal when you publish — common with a **Lifetime** bonus — Referly asks whether to pay them right away. See [Award a bonus retroactively](/help-center/gamify/performance-bonuses/retroactive).
</Note>

## Choosing the audience

![pb-audience](https://qnthgdfusotvjgaxpfie.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/public-assets/docs/uploads/1784760615079-191536-SCR-20260723-cmzi.png)

- **All affiliates** — everyone active in your program can earn it.
- **Specific affiliates** — search for and pick individual affiliates; only they can earn it.
- **Affiliate groups** — pick one or more [groups](/help-center/manage/affiliates/groups); only affiliates in those groups can earn it.

## Letting affiliates know

When you publish, Referly may offer to **send an announcement email** to the affiliates who can earn the bonus, so they hear about it straight away. Choose to send it or skip — either way the bonus is live. You can also count on it appearing on their portal's Performance Bonuses page.

## Save a draft

If you want to finish a bonus later, or launch it at a time of your choosing, select **Save draft** next to **Publish** at the top of the editor. You'll see **Draft saved**, and the bonus is added to your list switched off.

A draft works differently from a published bonus:

- Affiliates can't see it or earn it while it's switched off.
- Referly doesn't show the retroactive award prompt, even if some affiliates already meet the goal.
- Referly doesn't offer to send the announcement email.

When you're ready, switch the bonus on from its card on the **Performance Bonuses** page. See [Turning a bonus on and off](/help-center/gamify/performance-bonuses/overview#turning-a-bonus-on-and-off).

![The Unsaved changes bar at the bottom of the performance bonus editor with the Discard and Save draft buttons](/images/help-center/gamify/pb-save-draft.png)

## Live bonus limits

Your plan sets how many performance bonuses can be live at once, counted across every program in your account. Only bonuses that are switched on count toward the limit, so drafts and bonuses you've turned off don't use it up.

The limit is checked when a bonus goes live: when you select **Publish**, or when you switch a bonus on from its card. If you're already at your limit, you'll see an upgrade prompt instead. You can still save new bonuses as drafts on any plan, and switch one on once you have room, for example after turning another bonus off.

The **Gamify add-on** removes the limit, so you can run as many live bonuses as you like. See [billing and plans](/help-center/billing/plans-and-features) and [add-ons](/help-center/billing/add-ons).

## Editing a bonus later

Open any bonus from the list to change its name, criteria, audience, or schedule. While editing, a **Given Bonuses** tab shows the affiliates who've already earned it. Changing the criteria can bring new affiliates into range — including ones who already qualify, which again triggers the retroactive prompt on save.
