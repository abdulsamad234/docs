When you set up a [performance bonus](/help-center/gamify/performance-bonuses/overview), some of your affiliates may already have passed the goal — especially if you're counting their **Lifetime** activity. Referly doesn't make you chase those people down. When you save the bonus, it checks who already qualifies and offers to pay them on the spot.

## When the prompt appears

As you **Publish** a new bonus (or **Save Changes** to one you're editing), Referly looks at everyone in the bonus's audience and finds any who already meet the goal but haven't been paid it yet. If it finds some, an **Award bonuses now?** window opens before the bonus saves.

This comes up most often when:

- You choose the **Lifetime** scope, which counts everything an affiliate has done since they joined — so long-standing affiliates may already be over the target.
- You **edit an existing bonus** and lower the target or widen the audience, bringing already-qualified affiliates into range.

If nobody already qualifies, there's no prompt — the bonus just saves and waits for affiliates to reach the goal on their own.

## Awarding the bonus

The window shows exactly who's about to be paid and how far past the goal they are — for example, *12 / 10 referrals* — so there are no surprises. It lists the first several affiliates and tells you how many more there are.

You have two choices:

- **Save and award** — saves the bonus and pays every listed affiliate their reward right away. Each earned bonus lands in that affiliate's [rewards](/help-center/manage/rewards/bonuses) and triggers their **Performance Bonus Earned** notification, exactly as if they'd just hit the goal.
- **Cancel** — closes the window without saving. Nothing is awarded, and you can adjust the bonus first.

<Note>
  Each affiliate can only earn a given bonus once. Affiliates who were already paid this bonus won't appear in the prompt or be paid again.
</Note>

## After you award

Once awarded, these bonuses behave like any other earned reward — they carry an accepted status and are collected into [payouts](/help-center/manage/payouts/overview) along with the affiliate's commissions. From then on, the bonus keeps running: any affiliate who reaches the goal later is paid automatically, no prompt needed.
