Content rewards let you pay your affiliates and creators for the content they post about you — a TikTok, a YouTube video, an Instagram reel, a write-up. You set up a reward, affiliates publish and submit their post, you review it, and the reward lands in their earnings. It turns your affiliates into a content engine and pays them for the reach they bring.

You'll find it under **Content Rewards** in the left sidebar.

## The pieces

Four things work together:

- **Campaigns** — a reward offer you create, like "Post a YouTube video about us." A campaign sets the reward, who can take part, and how a post qualifies.
- **Submissions** — an affiliate's entry to a campaign: the post they published, plus any proof.
- **Criteria** — how a submission is judged and priced. Either **social metrics** (the reward is based on real views or likes the post earns) or **manual submission** (you review the proof and decide). See [Social metrics or manual review](/help-center/gamify/content-rewards/criteria).
- **Rewards** — when you approve a submission, its reward is paid to the affiliate as part of their earnings.

## How it works, end to end

<Steps>
  <Step title="You create a campaign">
    Set the reward, the criteria, the schedule, and who can take part, then publish it. See [Create a campaign](/help-center/gamify/content-rewards/campaigns).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Affiliates submit their content">
    Eligible affiliates see the campaign in their portal, publish a post, and submit it (for social campaigns, from an account they've connected and verified).
  </Step>
  <Step title="You review each submission">
    Every submission arrives as **Pending review**. You open it, check the post or proof, and **approve** or **reject** it. See [Review submissions](/help-center/gamify/content-rewards/submissions).
  </Step>
  <Step title="The reward is paid">
    Approving a submission creates its payout in the affiliate's rewards, ready to be paid out with everything else they've earned.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Submissions are never approved automatically — even on a social-metrics campaign, every entry waits for you to review it. Social metrics decide the reward *amount*; they don't approve the post for you.
</Note>

## Finding your way around

Content Rewards has three tabs:

- **Overview** — headline numbers for the whole feature: total views your content has earned, total payouts (split into paid and pending), and submission counts, with a chart and your most recent submissions.
- **Submissions** — every entry from every campaign, where you do your reviewing. A badge shows how many are waiting on you.
- **Campaigns** — all your campaigns, where you create and manage them.

## What your affiliates see

In their portal, affiliates get a **Content Rewards** page listing the campaigns they can take part in. Each card shows the reward on offer and a **Submit** button. When they submit, their entry shows as **Pending review** until you act on it, and once approved they can see what they earned. For social campaigns, they're prompted to connect and verify the right account before they can submit.

## Where rewards end up

An approved content reward becomes an entry in your [Rewards list](/help-center/manage/rewards/bonuses) labelled **Content rewards**, and is paid out the same way as commissions and bonuses. If you ever need to undo one, see [Revert a paid reward](/help-center/gamify/content-rewards/revert).
