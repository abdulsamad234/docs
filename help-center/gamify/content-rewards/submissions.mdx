When affiliates submit content, their entries wait for you to review them. Nothing is paid until you approve it, so this is where you check each post and decide. For the wider flow, see the [content rewards overview](/help-center/gamify/content-rewards/overview).

## Finding submissions to review

Open **Content Rewards** in the left sidebar and go to the **Submissions** tab. Its badge shows how many entries are waiting on you. The table lists every submission across your campaigns, with the creator, the platform, when the post went up, when it was submitted, the campaign, its views and likes, the payout, and its status. You can search and filter to narrow things down.

![cr-submissions-table](https://qnthgdfusotvjgaxpfie.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/public-assets/docs/uploads/1784761946218-155176-SCR-20260723-cuqr.png)

### What the statuses mean

Each submission carries a status:

- **Pending review** — waiting for you to approve or reject it. These are the ones that need action.
- **Approved** — you've accepted it and its reward has been created.
- **Rejected** — you've turned it down.
- **Draft** — the affiliate started an entry but hasn't submitted it yet.

The **Payout** column tells you where the money is: **Unpaid**, **Payout created** (the reward exists and is waiting to be paid out), or **Paid**.

## Reviewing one submission

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open it">
    Select a submission to open the **Review submission** panel. You can move through entries with the arrows at the top without closing the panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the details">
    The panel shows who submitted it, the campaign's criteria, and the reward at stake. For a **social** post you'll see a preview with its views and likes; you can **Refresh** to pull the latest figures. For a **manual** submission you'll see the proof they attached — images, links, and any notes they added.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve or reject">
    Use **Approve** to accept it or **Reject** to turn it down. These buttons appear while the submission is still pending review.
  </Step>
</Steps>

![cr-review-sheet](https://qnthgdfusotvjgaxpfie.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/public-assets/docs/uploads/1784761951257-304803-SCR-20260723-cuuu.png)

### Approving

Select **Approve** to accept the submission. Its reward is worked out and created right away in the affiliate's earnings.

If the campaign uses a **custom amount** (a manual campaign where you set the figure yourself), a **Reward amount** field appears first — type what to pay, then approve. For social campaigns and flat-rate campaigns, the amount is already worked out for you.

### Rejecting

Select **Reject**, then choose a **Reason** — Invalid proof, Duplicate submission, Out of time window, Did not meet criteria, or Other — and add optional details for the affiliate. Choose **Confirm rejection** to finish. The affiliate is told their submission was rejected, along with your reason.

![cr-reject-reason](https://qnthgdfusotvjgaxpfie.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/public-assets/docs/uploads/1784761954674-348676-SCR-20260723-cuzd.png)

## Reviewing several at once

When a batch is clearly good or bad, select the rows with their checkboxes and use the bar that appears to set them all to **Approved**, **Pending review**, or **Rejected** in one step — or delete them. You can also select every submission that matches your current filters.

## Where the reward goes

Approving a submission creates its reward in your [Rewards list](/help-center/manage/rewards/bonuses), labelled **Content rewards**, and from there it's paid out like any commission or bonus. On a social campaign with extra-performance rewards turned on, the amount can keep climbing after approval as the post gains views or likes. If you ever need to undo an approval, see [Revert a paid reward](/help-center/gamify/content-rewards/revert).
