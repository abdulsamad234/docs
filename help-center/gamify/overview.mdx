Commissions reward affiliates for the sales they drive. Gamification rewards everything around that — reaching a goal, out-performing the pack, growing with your program, and creating content about you. Used well, it gives your affiliates a reason to keep going and turns a flat program into something they want to win at.

Referly gives you five tools, each doing a different job. You'll find them in the left sidebar.

## Milestones

**Move affiliates onto better terms as they grow.** Set referral or revenue goals, and when an affiliate reaches one, Referly automatically upgrades them to a better commission plan. It's the ladder your affiliates climb — the more they bring in, the more generous their terms become. [Learn about milestones](/help-center/gamify/milestones/overview).

## Performance bonuses

**Pay a fixed reward for hitting a target.** Promise a set cash amount for a goal — 10 referrals, $5,000 in revenue, 25 sales — and it's paid automatically the moment an affiliate gets there. A clear target with a clear payout, great for short pushes and standing challenges alike. [Learn about performance bonuses](/help-center/gamify/performance-bonuses/overview).

## Content rewards

**Pay affiliates for the content they post about you.** Create campaigns that reward posts and videos — either by the real views and likes they earn, or by your own review of what they submit. It turns your affiliates into a content engine and pays them for the reach. [Learn about content rewards](/help-center/gamify/content-rewards/overview).

## Leaderboards

**Rank your affiliates by performance.** Show who's on top by referrals, revenue, sales, earnings, or conversion rate over a time window you choose. A little friendly competition motivates the middle of the pack, and you can publish a leaderboard as a public page to help recruit new affiliates. [Learn about leaderboards](/help-center/gamify/leaderboards/overview).

## Contests

**Run a time-boxed competition with prizes.** Set a metric, a schedule, and cash or non-cash prizes; Referly picks the winners automatically at the end of each period. Contests turn the leaderboard into a race with a finish line and a reward. [Learn about contests](/help-center/gamify/contests/overview).

## Choosing where to start

You don't have to use all five, and they work well together. A quick way to think about it:

- Want to **reward loyalty and growth** over time? Start with **milestones**.
- Want to **drive a specific push** with a clear payout? Use a **performance bonus** or a **contest**.
- Want **more people talking about you**? Run a **content rewards** campaign.
- Want to **spark competition** and show off your top affiliates? Turn on a **leaderboard**.

<Note>
  Some gamification features and higher usage limits are part of the Gamification add-on or higher plans. See [plans and features](/help-center/billing/plans-and-features) for what's included on yours.
</Note>
