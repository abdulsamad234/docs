Your leaderboard doesn't have to live inside the affiliate portal. Referly gives every program a **public leaderboard page** — a branded, standalone page anyone can view without logging in. It's a simple way to show momentum, celebrate your top affiliates, and give prospective affiliates a reason to join.

The public page is turned **on by default**, so your leaderboard is already shareable. This page covers how to open it, what visitors see, and how to control privacy or switch it off.

## Open your public page

Open **Leaderboards** from the left sidebar. When the public page is on, a **View public page** link appears next to the leaderboard title. Select it to open the page exactly as visitors see it, then copy the address from your browser to share it anywhere — your website, social posts, or a recruiting email.

## What visitors see

The public page shows your rankings wrapped in your program's branding — your logo and accent color. Visitors can:

- Switch the **time window** to see today's, this week's, or all-time standings.
- Filter the board by **country**.
- Follow a **join** link to sign up as an affiliate, so the page doubles as a recruiting tool.

They don't need an account, and they only ever see the rankings — never your dashboard or any private program data.

![public-leaderboard-page](https://qnthgdfusotvjgaxpfie.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/public-assets/docs/uploads/1784758824408-884159-SCR-20260723-cclz.png)

## Keep affiliate identities private

By default, the public page **masks affiliate names and emails**, so visitors see the standings and the metrics without seeing exactly who each affiliate is. This is usually what you want for a page anyone can reach.

To change it, open the leaderboard settings from the gear icon in the toolbar and find **Hide affiliate info from public leaderboard** under **Privacy & placement**. Turn it off only if you're confident your affiliates are happy to be named publicly.

## Turn the public page off

If you'd rather keep your leaderboard inside the portal only, open the leaderboard settings and turn off **Show the leaderboard publicly**. The public page immediately stops being reachable and the **View public page** link disappears.

<Note>
  Hiding the public leaderboard requires the **Gamification add-on** or an Enterprise plan. Without it, the public page stays on. See [Add-ons](/help-center/billing/add-ons).
</Note>
