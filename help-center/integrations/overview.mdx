Referly can only reward the right affiliate if it knows when a referred visitor actually becomes a customer. Integrations are how that information reaches Referly: you connect the tool you take payment with, and you add a small piece of tracking to your checkout or your site. From then on, every referred sale shows up in your dashboard and turns into a commission on its own.

<Note>
  Looking for your ad platform pixels (Meta, Google Ads, TikTok and more) and server-side conversion events? Those are part of [Referly Atlas](/help-center/atlas), which has its own [pixel guides](/help-center/atlas/pixels).
</Note>

This page is the map. It covers where integrations live, the two pieces every setup needs, and which guide to open for your platform.

![Referly connected to its integrations](/images/integrations/overview/hero.png)

## Where to find your integrations

In the left sidebar, select **Settings**, then **Integrations**. You land on **Connected integrations**, which shows everything linked to your program in six sections:

- **Tracking script**: the Referly script on your website, which records clicks and referrals.
- **Referral tracking**: where Referly learns about sign-ups, free trials and first purchases.
- **Sales tracking**: where Referly learns about purchases, so commissions can be worked out.
- **Coupons**: affiliate coupon codes created and tracked through your payment processor.
- **Payouts**: the providers you pay your affiliates through.
- **Email integrations**: email tools your new affiliates are added to automatically.

![The Connected integrations page with the numbered Tracking script, Referral tracking and Sales tracking sections and their Add integration cards](/images/help-center/integrations/integrations-page-v2.png)

Every section except **Tracking script** ends with a card for adding another integration, such as **Add integration** or **Connect payout method**. If you'd rather be walked through the whole setup in order, select **Setup Integration** at the top right.

The sidebar also has **All integrations**, which opens the full list of Referly integrations on the Referly website in a new tab. If an agency runs your program, it may hide this link.

## The two pieces of an integration

Most setups need both halves, and they do different jobs:

1. **A connection to your payment provider.** This is what lets Referly read your sales and refunds, so it knows the real amount of every order and can work out the commission. For some providers it also lets Referly create the discount codes your affiliates hand out.
2. **Tracking on your site or checkout.** This is what tells Referly *which* affiliate a given sale belongs to. Without it, Referly sees the sale but has no idea who sent the customer.

The one common exception is a coupon-only program. If you track referrals purely through discount codes, each affiliate gets their own code and the code itself identifies them, so there's no script to add to your site.

## Connect your payment provider

In the **Sales tracking** section, select **Add integration**, then pick your provider from the list (you can search for it by name). Its setup steps open, and for most providers the first step has a button such as **Connect Stripe** that opens the provider's connection panel.

![The Track your sales step of the integration setup with the search box and provider tiles for Stripe, Shopify, WooCommerce, Chargebee, Paddle, Polar, Creem and Sharetribe](/images/help-center/integrations/choose-integration-v2.png)

You can connect **Stripe**, **Shopify**, **Chargebee**, **Paddle**, **WooCommerce**, **Polar**, **Creem**, **Ecwid**, and **Sharetribe**. A few integrations, like **Zylvie**, are private and only appear once they've been enabled for your account — contact support if you need one of those.

How you connect depends on the provider:

- **Stripe** and **Shopify** connect by signing in and approving Referly. There's nothing to copy and paste. Stripe also offers a test mode connection if you want to try the flow before going live.
- **Ecwid** connects when you install the Referly app from the Ecwid App Market. See the [Ecwid guide](/help-center/integrations/ecwid).
- **Chargebee**, **Paddle**, **WooCommerce**, **Polar**, **Creem**, and **Sharetribe** ask for credentials from that tool, such as an API key or access token. Referly tells you exactly which fields it needs and which permissions to switch on before you generate the key, so open that panel first and follow it in order.

Once a provider is linked, it shows a green **Connected** badge along with the account or site it's connected to, so you can tell at a glance that it worked.

![connected-provider](https://qnthgdfusotvjgaxpfie.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/public-assets/docs/uploads/1784804395241-996589-final-connected-provider-1784804394992.png)

To unlink a provider, open the same panel and select **Disconnect**.

## Choose the right setup guide

Every integration you pick under **Referral tracking** or **Sales tracking** comes with its own step-by-step guide. The steps open in the order you do them, with your program's own tracking code already filled in, so there's nothing to assemble yourself.

![The Stripe setup guide inside the integration setup, with step 1 Connect Stripe and step 2 Set up sales tracking showing the method dropdown and the code snippet](/images/help-center/integrations/instructions-dialog-v2.png)

Steps Referly can check for itself, such as connecting your account or verifying your tracking script, are ticked off on their own. For the rest, select **Mark as completed** once you've done them.

When a platform can be set up in more than one way, a dropdown at the top of the step lets you choose the method. Pick the one that matches **how you take payment**, not only which company processes it. Stripe alone has five methods, because a Stripe Payment Link and a Stripe Pricing Table are set up in completely different ways. If you're not sure, check your checkout page and see which one it resembles.

![The method dropdown at the top of a Stripe setup step, open to show Checkout Sessions API, Coupon codes, Payment Links, Pricing Table and Buy Button](/images/help-center/integrations/guide-card-chips-v2.png)

## If your platform isn't on the list

You don't need a named integration to run a program. Four options cover almost everything else:

- **Universal Forms Integration** tracks referrals through any form on your website, with no payment processor involved. It's the simplest option if you only need to know who referred a sign-up.
- **Zapier** connects Referly to more than 8,000 apps, and **Make** to more than 2,400, so you can pass sales in from a tool Referly doesn't talk to directly.
- **The Referly API** gives a developer full control over how sales are reported. It's the most flexible route and the most involved.

## Integrations set up somewhere else

Two kinds of connection have their own settings, because they're part of a different job. You can also connect both from the **Email integrations** and **Payouts** sections of the Integrations page:

- **Email marketing.** Klaviyo, Mailchimp, MailerLite, and Brevo sync your affiliates into a mailing list so you can send them campaigns. Connect these under **Settings**, then **Affiliate portal**, then **Email settings**. See [email marketing integrations](/help-center/engage/emails/integrations).
- **Payout methods.** Wise, PayPal, and Referly Payouts are how you actually send affiliates their money. Set these up under **Settings**, then **Program details**, then **Payout settings**. See [payout methods](/help-center/manage/payouts/payout-methods).
