The referral widget puts your affiliate program right inside your product. Instead of sending people off to a separate portal, you add a small floating button to your site. When a customer selects it, a panel opens where they can grab their referral link, copy a coupon, share on social, invite friends by email, and see what they've earned — all without leaving your app. It's the quickest way to turn the customers you already have into affiliates.

This article is a tour of what the widget is and what it does. The hands-on tasks — installing it, customizing it, and how affiliates verify their email — each have their own article, linked at the end.

<Info>
  The referral widget is available on the Business plan and higher. See [plans and features](/help-center/billing/plans-and-features) for what each plan includes.
</Info>

## What your customers see

The widget shows up as a floating button in a corner of your site. Selecting it opens a panel with two tabs, **Invite** and **Earnings**.

### The Invite tab

This is where an affiliate gets everything they need to start promoting you:

- Their personal **referral link**, ready to copy.
- Their **coupon code**, if you've attached one to the widget.
- Buttons to share straight to X, LinkedIn, or email, and to show a QR code for sharing on mobile.
- An **Invite Friends** option that lets them email up to ten friends at once, with a personal note.

{/* TODO screenshot: widget-invite-tab — upload it in the local Documentation Agent */}

### The Earnings tab

The Earnings tab shows an affiliate how they're doing — their total earned, how many people they've referred, and how many clicks their link has had. From here they can also add their payout details so they can get paid.

## The invitation popup

The widget has a second job. When someone lands on your site through an affiliate's referral link, you can greet them with an **invitation popup** — a small welcome that shows who invited them and the discount they've unlocked. It's a warm first impression that nudges new visitors to sign up.

{/* TODO screenshot: invitation-popup — upload it in the local Documentation Agent */}

You control the greeting, the discount message, the "invited by" line, and an optional call-to-action button. All of this is covered in [Customize the widget](/help-center/engage/widget/customize).

## Where you set it up

Everything for the widget lives in one place. From your campaign dashboard, open **Widget** in the left sidebar. The screen has two tabs:

{/* TODO screenshot: widget-dashboard-tabs — upload it in the local Documentation Agent */}

- **Edit Widget** is where you turn the widget on, choose its colors and position, write its text, pick a coupon, and set up the invitation popup.
- **Analytics** shows how the widget is performing — how many times it's been opened, how many links and coupons were copied, how many invitations went out, and which affiliates are driving the most activity.
