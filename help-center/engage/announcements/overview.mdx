Announcements let you broadcast a one-way message to the people promoting your product, shown right inside their affiliate portal. Use them to launch a new promotion, share a program change, or point affiliates to a fresh set of marketing assets — without sending a single email.

Unlike [Messages](/help-center/engage/messages/overview), which are back-and-forth conversations, an announcement is a broadcast: you publish it once and every affiliate in your chosen audience sees it. This page is a tour of how announcements work and where each setting lives. Each part links to a deeper guide when you want more detail.

## Where announcements appear

Announcements show up inside the [affiliate portal](/help-center/affiliates/portal-overview) — the dashboard your affiliates log in to. Every announcement is a **pop-up**: a card that opens over the affiliate's dashboard, so it's hard to miss. Your affiliates don't need to do anything to receive one; the next time they open their portal, it's there.

<Note>
Pop-ups are the only announcement format available today. More formats may follow, but for now every announcement you create is a pop-up.
</Note>

## Open Announcements

In the left sidebar, select **Affiliate Announcements**. The page opens with your list of announcements and a calendar that shows when each one is scheduled or running. To start a new one, select **Create Announcement** in the top right.

![announcements-list](https://qnthgdfusotvjgaxpfie.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/public-assets/docs/uploads/1784752013020-470117-SCR-20260722-uesc.png)

## Build it in the editor

When you create or open an announcement, the editor has three tabs, with a live preview beside them so you can see your changes as you make them:

- **Setup** — controls *when*, *where*, and *who*: how it's delivered, how often it shows, which portal pages it appears on, the audience that receives it, and who it appears to be from.
- **Design & Content** — the words and look: layout, title, body, image, colours, button, and position on the screen.
- **Results** — how the announcement is performing once it's live.

![announcement-editor-tabs](https://qnthgdfusotvjgaxpfie.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/public-assets/docs/uploads/1784752025713-877930-SCR-20260722-ufch.png)

For everything on the Design & Content tab — layouts, images, the call-to-action button and the countdown timer — see [Pop-up settings](/help-center/engage/announcements/types).

## Choose how it's sent

Each announcement is delivered in one of two ways, which you pick at the top of the **Setup** tab:

- **Campaign** — a one-time send. You publish it now or schedule it for a future date and time, and it goes out to your audience once.
- **Automation** — a rule that sends the announcement automatically whenever an affiliate meets a condition, such as a set number of days after they join. New affiliates receive it when they qualify, without you lifting a finger.

![announcement-delivery-choice](https://qnthgdfusotvjgaxpfie.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/public-assets/docs/uploads/1784765996742-413545-SCR-20260723-dssu.png)

## Decide who sees it

By default an announcement goes to everyone in your program. You can also narrow it to a specific group — for example only your top performers, affiliates in a certain group, or people who joined recently. See [Target an audience](/help-center/engage/announcements/audience) for the full set of targeting options.

## Control when and how often it shows

On the **Setup** tab you can schedule an announcement to go live at a future date, set how many times it reappears after an affiliate dismisses it, and choose how long to wait between each showing. You can also limit it to specific pages of the portal, so it only appears where it's relevant.

### Set a display window

In the **When** section of the **Setup** tab, use **Display window** to choose the dates the pop-up can appear between:

- **Starts**: when the pop-up can begin showing. Left empty, it reads **Immediately**.
- **Ends**: when the pop-up stops showing. Left empty, it reads **Never**, so the pop-up keeps running.

Outside the window the pop-up doesn't appear, but affiliates can still find the announcement in their notification inbox. Once the end date passes, the announcement can't be turned back on, so choose an end date you're sure about.

The end date has to be after the start date. If you pick an end date that has already passed, the editor warns you that the announcement won't display, and you'll need to change it before you can publish.

![The When section of the announcement editor with the Display window card, its Starts and Ends date fields and the explanation that the popup only appears between these dates](/images/help-center/engage/announcement-display-window.png)

## Track results

Once an announcement is live, open its **Results** tab to see how many affiliates it was **sent** to, how many **viewed** it, how many **clicked** its button, and how many **dismissed** it. Use this to tell whether a promotion landed or whether it's worth sending a follow-up.

![announcement-results](https://qnthgdfusotvjgaxpfie.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/public-assets/docs/uploads/1784766003061-100130-SCR-20260723-dsxp.png)

## Statuses and the calendar

Every announcement carries a status badge so you always know where it stands. Campaigns show:

- **Draft**: still being built; not visible to anyone.
- **Scheduled**: set to go out at a future date. To hold it back, open the card's menu and select **Suspend**, which returns it to **Draft**.
- **Sent**: published to your audience.

Automations show **Active** while the automation is switched on, **Inactive** when it's switched off, and **Draft** before you publish it.

Two more badges take priority over the ones above once an announcement is published:

- **Off**: you turned the announcement off, so it no longer pops up. It stays in your affiliates' notification inboxes.
- **Expired**: its display window has ended, so it won't pop up again.

Under each announcement's name, a line shows when it was created, scheduled, or sent. If it has an end date, the line adds **Shows until** and that date, or **Stopped showing** and the date once the window has ended.

The calendar on the Announcements page plots your scheduled and active announcements by day, so you can see what's going out and when at a glance.

![announcement-status-badges](https://qnthgdfusotvjgaxpfie.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/public-assets/docs/uploads/1784752032684-902907-SCR-20260722-ufkh.png)

### Turn an announcement on or off

Each campaign that's scheduled or sent has a switch on its card in the list. Turn it off to stop the pop-up from appearing. The announcement stays in your affiliates' notification inboxes, so anyone who already received it can still read it there. Turn it back on whenever you like, as long as its display window hasn't ended.

Once the display window has ended, the switch is greyed out and the announcement can't be turned back on. Hover over the switch to see why.

Automations have their own switch on the card, which turns the automation on or off.

![Three announcement cards, each with its on-off switch, type badge, sent date and delivery stats](/images/help-center/engage/announcement-on-off-switch.png)

## Availability

Announcements are part of Referly's engagement features. Depending on your plan, announcements and announcement automations may require the Engagement add-on. See [Add-ons](/help-center/billing/add-ons) for what's included.
