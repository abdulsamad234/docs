Content rewards pay you for posting, not for selling. The brand runs a campaign, you make something and put it live, then you submit it and get paid — either a set amount for taking part or an amount that grows with how many views your post gets.

It's separate from your commission. You can earn a content reward and commission on the same post.

## Find the campaigns you can enter

In the left sidebar of the affiliate portal, select **Content Rewards**. Every campaign open to you appears as a card.

![content-rewards-page](https://qnthgdfusotvjgaxpfie.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/public-assets/docs/uploads/1784819065142-909603-final-content-rewards-page-1784819064902.png)

Each card shows what you can earn, how many submissions you've used out of your allowance, and when the campaign ends. If the page says there are no campaigns available right now, the brand either hasn't launched one or hasn't opened this one to you.

Select a card to open it.

## Read the rules before you post

This is the step worth slowing down for. Once your post is live it's hard to change, and a post that misses a requirement gets turned down.

Inside a campaign, the **How it works** panel lists everything the brand has set:

![campaign-detail-how-it-works](https://qnthgdfusotvjgaxpfie.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/public-assets/docs/uploads/1784819063696-754798-final-campaign-detail-how-it-works-1784819063353.png)

| What it says | What it means for you |
| --- | --- |
| **Platform** | Where your post has to be |
| **Measured by** | Whether your reward is based on views or likes |
| **Submissions allowed** | How many posts you can enter |
| **How often** | The gap you have to leave between submissions |
| **Starts** and **Ends** | The life of the campaign |
| **Submission window** | Some campaigns only accept entries in the last few days |
| **Reward budget** | The total pot, which closes the campaign once it runs out |
| **Proof required** | What you have to attach, for campaigns judged by hand |
| **Max links** and **Accepted links from** | Limits on the links you can include |

Read the campaign description above it too. That's where the brand puts the creative brief, the hashtags and anything they want you to say or avoid.

<Warning>
  The reward budget is shared across everyone in the campaign. When it's used up the campaign closes, even if the end date hasn't arrived. If a campaign matters to you, enter it early.
</Warning>

## Connect your social account

Campaigns measured by views or likes need to know the post is really yours. The first time you try to submit to one, you'll be asked to connect and verify the account you post from.

![connect-social-account](https://qnthgdfusotvjgaxpfie.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/public-assets/docs/uploads/1784818377734-653422-final-connect-social-account-1784818377631.png)

Select the connect button and follow the prompts on the platform. You come straight back to the campaign afterwards, ready to submit.

You only do this once per platform. After that, every campaign on that platform lets you submit right away.

<Note>
  Submit posts from the account you connected. A post from a different account, or from an account you haven't verified, can't be matched to you.
</Note>

## Submit your post

Select **Submit** on the campaign. What you fill in depends on how the brand set it up.

### For a campaign measured by views or likes

Paste the address of your live post into the post field and select **Submit**. That's the whole form.

![submit-social-post](https://qnthgdfusotvjgaxpfie.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/public-assets/docs/uploads/1784818381780-967374-final-submit-social-post-1784818381688.png)

Grab the address from the post itself rather than from your profile, so it points at the single post. Once it's in, the view and like counts are picked up automatically and refreshed over time, so you don't have to report numbers yourself.

LinkedIn works slightly differently: instead of the address, the form asks for the post's embed code. Open your post on LinkedIn, select the **•••** menu at its top right, choose **Embed this post**, copy the code, and paste it in.

### For a campaign judged by hand

Some campaigns ask you to show your work instead of tracking a metric. The form asks for whichever of these the brand requires:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Attach images">
    Upload a screenshot or photo as proof. Files can be SVG, JPG, PNG or WEBP and no larger than 5MB each.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add links">
    Paste the address of what you posted. Use **Add URL** for more than one. If the brand only accepts links from certain sites, those are listed under the field.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add any details">
    An optional box for explaining what you did. Worth filling in — it's what the reviewer reads first.
  </Step>
</Steps>

![submit-manual-proof](https://qnthgdfusotvjgaxpfie.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/public-assets/docs/uploads/1784818381034-511983-final-submit-manual-proof-1784818380877.png)

**Save progress** keeps what you've entered as a draft so you can come back and finish it. A draft doesn't use up one of your submissions and isn't seen by the brand until you select **Submit**.

## Track what happens next

After you submit, your entry appears under **My submissions** inside the campaign, with its current views or likes, what it's earned, and where it's up to.

![my-submissions-list](https://qnthgdfusotvjgaxpfie.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/public-assets/docs/uploads/1784818378835-146125-final-my-submissions-list-1784818378658.png)

| Status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Draft** | Saved but not sent. The brand can't see it yet |
| **Pending review** | Sent. Waiting on the brand |
| **Approved** | Accepted. Your reward is counted |
| **Rejected** | Turned down, with a reason attached |

Select any submission to open its full detail, including a preview of the post, whatever proof you attached, and the amount earned.

![submission-detail-sheet](https://qnthgdfusotvjgaxpfie.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/public-assets/docs/uploads/1784818379569-571957-final-submission-detail-sheet-1784818379367.png)

If a submission was turned down, the reason shows here — **Invalid proof**, **Duplicate submission**, **Out of time window**, **Did not meet criteria** or **Other** — along with any note the reviewer left. Read the note before trying again, because it usually says exactly what to change.

<Warning>
  A rejected submission still uses one of your allowed submissions. You can't edit it and resubmit it. If you have submissions left you can enter a new post, but if you were only allowed one, that's the campaign done. This is the reason to check the rules before you post.
</Warning>

## How your reward is worked out

There are three shapes a reward can take, and the campaign tells you which one applies at the top of the page.

- **A set amount on approval.** You get the same figure whatever the post does, as soon as the brand approves it.
- **Something other than money.** Some campaigns offer a product or a perk instead, described in the reward line.
- **An amount that grows with your numbers.** You earn a base reward once the post passes a threshold, such as 10,000 views. If the brand added a bonus on top, you earn an extra amount for every so many views beyond that, sometimes up to a ceiling.

Where a campaign lets you submit more than one post, the reward normally applies **per post**. The **Earn up to** figure on the card is your allowance multiplied by the most a single post can earn.

Your running total for the campaign is shown as **Your earnings**, next to what the brand has paid out across everyone.

Once a submission is approved, the amount becomes a reward on your account. From there it follows the normal path: see [rewards and how you get paid](/help-center/affiliates/payouts).

## If you can't submit right now

When the **Submit** button is unavailable, the reason is written underneath it.

![submit-blocked-reason](https://qnthgdfusotvjgaxpfie.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/public-assets/docs/uploads/1784818380296-99588-final-submit-blocked-reason-1784818380130.png)

- **This content reward hasn't started yet** — come back on the start date.
- **This content reward has ended** — the end date has passed.
- **Submissions open x days before it ends** — the brand only accepts entries near the finish.
- **This content reward has used up its reward budget** — the pot has run out.
- **Limit reached** — you've used all your submissions.
- **You can only submit once a day** (or week, or month) — you're inside the cooldown after your last one.

## Find campaigns across every program

The Influencer dashboard at [influencers.referly.so](https://influencers.referly.so) has a **Content Rewards** section in the **Marketplace** that gathers campaigns from every brand on the network, not only the ones you've already joined.

![influencer-content-rewards-marketplace](https://qnthgdfusotvjgaxpfie.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/public-assets/docs/uploads/1784819066259-789288-final-influencer-content-rewards-marketplace-1784819065998.png)

Search it, filter by platform, or sort by how much a program has paid out so far. Open any campaign to see the same reward details and rules as inside a portal.

If a campaign belongs to a program you're not in yet, you'll see a **Join** button. Joining signs you up to that program and drops you straight into the campaign, ready to submit.

This is the fastest way to find paid content work you're eligible for. More on the dashboard in [your programs](/help-center/affiliates/my-programs).
