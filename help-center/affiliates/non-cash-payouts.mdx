Not every program pays in money. Some reward affiliates with a discount coupon, and others with store credit or points you can spend with them. Referly calls these **non-cash rewards**, and they reach you a little differently from cash.

This page is for you if your program rewards you this way. If you're paid in money, see [Cash payouts](/help-center/affiliates/cash-payouts) instead. Some programs use both.

## Set your non-cash rewards email

Open **Settings** in the left sidebar, choose the **Payouts** tab, then **Non-Cash Payouts**.

There's one setting here: **Non-Cash Rewards Email**. Enter the address where you'd like coupon codes and credits to reach you in the **Email Address** field, then select **Save Changes**. It starts out as the email on your account, so you only need to change it if you'd rather use a different one.

<Warning>
  This address does more than receive a message. When a program gives you store credit, the credit is often added to the customer account that matches this email in their store. Use the same address you use as a customer with them, or the credit may not find you.
</Warning>

You don't need to add bank details, a Wise account, or a PayPal address to receive non-cash rewards. Those are only for cash.

## Coupon rewards

When you earn a coupon, the program's store creates a real discount code for you and it becomes available in your portal. What the code is worth, and how long it lasts, is set by the program.

### Find and copy a code

Open **Payouts** in the left sidebar and look in your payout history for a row with a purple coupon chip in the **Reward** column. Select that row to open **Coupon Details**.

For each coupon you'll see:

- **Code** — the code itself, with a copy button next to it.
- **Discount** — what it takes off, for example 20% off or $10 off.
- A badge showing **Redeemed** or **Not Redeemed**, so you can tell at a glance which codes you've already used.

### See the terms of a coupon

Open **Rewards** in the left sidebar to see the individual sale each coupon came from. In the **Reward** column, a coupon shows as a purple chip summarising the discount and how long it lasts, such as `20% off · 3 months` or `$10 off · One-time use`.

Hover the chip for the full picture: the **Discount**, the **Source** it was created in, the **Coupon** name, and its **Duration**.

## Credit rewards

Credits are an amount added to your account with the program. Every program names its own unit, so you might see points, coins, store credit, or something specific to that company.

On the **Rewards** page, credits show as a green chip with the amount and unit, for example `500 points`. Hovering it shows the amount again and the **Source** the credit comes from. Where that credit actually lands depends on the program: it may be store credit on your customer account with them, a balance on their payment system, a single-use code sent to your non-cash rewards email, or something on their own platform they top up by hand.

If you can't find credit you've been awarded, check that your non-cash rewards email matches the account you use as their customer, then message the program through **Messages** in the left sidebar.

## When your rewards reach you

Non-cash rewards travel the same route as cash, with two useful differences.

Like cash, they still have to be **approved** by the program, and they're still gathered up on the program's payout schedule rather than the instant you earn them. A reward sitting at **On Hold** on your Rewards page hasn't been approved yet. See [How you get paid](/help-center/affiliates/payouts) for the full journey.

Unlike cash, they aren't held back by a minimum payout amount. A program can set a threshold you have to reach before money is sent, but that threshold applies only to cash, so a single coupon or a small amount of credit reaches you on schedule regardless.

On the **Payouts** page, your coupons and credits are counted separately from money. Small chips under **Total Earned**, **Total Paid**, **Next Payout**, and **Last Payout** show how many coupons and how much credit each figure includes, so the cash number stays readable.

## If you're in more than one program

Sign in to the Influencer dashboard at `influencers.referly.so` with the same email you use for your programs, and open **Payouts**. The summary cards add up across every program you've joined, coupons and credits included, and a **Program** column shows which program each reward came from.

Opening a coupon row there works exactly as it does in a single program's portal, so you can copy any code without switching accounts. See [My programs](/help-center/affiliates/my-programs) for more.
