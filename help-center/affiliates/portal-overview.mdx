Your affiliate portal is where you do the work of being an affiliate: grab your link, watch clicks turn into sales, and get paid. If you promote more than one brand on Referly, you also get an **Influencer dashboard** that pulls every program you've joined into a single login.

This page is a tour of both — what each one is for, how to move between them, and what every section and number means.

## The two places you log in

They're two views of the same account. The Influencer dashboard doesn't replace program portals — it sits above them and hands you off to the right one.

### A program portal

Every brand running a program on Referly gets its own affiliate portal. The address looks like `brandname.referly.so`, or the brand's own web address if they've set up a custom domain — so it may not say "Referly" anywhere.

The brand controls the logo, the colors, and which languages are available, which is why no two portals look quite the same. What stays the same is the layout and where things live.

Everything inside a program portal is scoped to that one program: your referral link for that brand, the commissions you earned there, and the payouts that brand owes you.

### Your Influencer dashboard

Go to `influencers.referly.so` and sign in with the same email you use for your programs. This is the cross-program view, and its sidebar is deliberately short:

- **My Programs** — a card for every program you've joined, with your total earnings and an earnings trend for each.
- **Marketplace** — browse and join new programs on the Referly network. See [My programs](/help-center/affiliates/my-programs).
- **Messages** — every conversation with every program team, in one inbox.
- **Payouts** — all your payouts across every program, with the program name on each row.
- **Settings** — your profile, payout details, and notification preferences.

Use it when you want the big picture or you're looking for new programs. Use a program portal when you want to actually promote, dig into performance, or check a specific brand's payouts.

## Move between your programs

At the very top of the left sidebar there's a button showing the current program's logo and name. Select it to open the **program switcher**.

Inside you'll find a search box, a **Your Programs** list of everything you've joined with a checkmark next to the one you're in, and **Join other programs** at the bottom, which takes you to the marketplace.

Picking a program from the list takes you straight to that brand's portal.

{/* TODO screenshot: program-switcher — upload it in the local Documentation Agent */}

From **My Programs** in your Influencer dashboard you can do the same thing from a program's card. Select the **...** button in the card's top-right corner for:

- **Go to program** — opens that brand's portal in a new tab, already signed in.
- **Promote** — opens your link and promo code for that program without leaving the page.
- **Commission plan** — shows exactly what that program pays you.

## Find your way around a program portal

The left sidebar is the same set of sections in every program portal:

- **Dashboard** — your performance at a glance. Covered in detail below.
- **Referrals** — every customer who signed up or bought through your link, with their revenue, your commission, and their status.
- **Rewards** — every individual commission you've earned, and whether it's been approved and paid.
- **Messages** — your conversation with the program's team. A pill shows unread messages.
- **Leaderboard** — where you rank against other affiliates. It's labelled **Contests** instead when the brand is running one. See [Leaderboards and contests](/help-center/affiliates/leaderboards).
- **Performance Bonuses** — extra rewards you can unlock by hitting targets. See [Performance bonuses](/help-center/affiliates/performance-bonuses).
- **Content Rewards** — get paid for posting content. See [Content rewards](/help-center/affiliates/content-rewards).
- **Marketing Assets** — the banners, images, and files the brand has shared with you. See [Marketing assets](/help-center/affiliates/assets).
- **My Team** — the affiliates you've recruited, when the program pays you on their sales. See [My team](/help-center/affiliates/my-team).
- **Payouts** — what you've earned, what's been paid, and what's coming. See [Getting paid](/help-center/affiliates/payouts).
- **Settings** — your profile, links, payout method, and notifications.

Along the top bar you'll find your current **Commission Rate**, a **Promote** button, a notification bell, a language picker, and a light/dark toggle.

The **Promote** button is the fastest route to your link. It opens **Your Affiliate Details**, where you can copy your full referral link, switch to a short version, generate or print a QR code, and see your discount code if the brand gave you one. ## Read the numbers on your dashboard

Start with the **date range picker** in the top right. Everything on the page follows it, so a number that looks wrong is usually just a date range that isn't what you expected. Presets run from **Today** through **This year**, plus **Maximum** for everything since you joined and **Custom Range** for your own dates.

### The Overview card

The big card shows one metric at a time. Use the picker to switch between:

- **Clicks** — how many times someone opened your referral link.
- **Referrals** — how many of those visitors became customers of the brand.
- **Earnings** — the commission those customers generated for you.

Under the big number is the percentage change **from last period** — the same length of time immediately before your selected range. Green means you're up on last period, red means you're down.

Two extra toggles appear depending on the metric:

- On **Clicks**, choose **All** or **Unique**. Unique counts one click per person, so it's the honest measure of how many individuals you reached.
- On **Earnings**, choose **All**, **New**, or **Recurring**. New is commission from a customer's first purchase only. Recurring is everything they buy after that — the sign that your referrals are sticking around.

The chart underneath plots the same metric over time. Switch between **Hourly**, **Daily**, **Weekly**, and **Monthly** to zoom in or out.

### The Conversion Funnel

The funnel shows where people drop off on the way to becoming a sale:

**Clicks** → **Referrals** → **Conversions**

On some programs the middle step is **Add to Carts** instead of Referrals, depending on what the brand tracks. Between each step is a conversion rate, so you can see whether your problem is getting traffic, getting sign-ups, or getting purchases.

The **Conversion Type** control filters the last step to **All**, **New** (first-time purchases only), or **Recurring** (repeat purchases).

### The supporting cards

- **Recent Earnings** — your latest commissions, with the customer and whether a promo code was involved.
- **Top Traffic Sources** — where your clicks are coming from, sortable by clicks, referrals, or earnings. Traffic with no identifiable source shows as **Direct / None**.
- **Recent Payouts** — the last payments sent to you.
- **Action Items** and **Quick Links** — shortcuts to your referrals, rewards, marketing assets, links and promo codes, and your commission plan.

## Know where each reward stands

A commission doesn't go straight to your bank. It passes through two separate states, and it's worth knowing which one you're looking at.

### On the Rewards page

Each row is one commission, with columns for **Customer**, **Revenue**, **Reward**, **Type**, **Date**, **Payout**, and **Status**. The status tells you whether the brand has cleared it:

- **Accepted** — approved, and eligible to be included in a payout.
- **On Hold** — waiting on the brand's review. It won't be paid until it's approved.
- **Refunded** — the customer got their money back, so this reward won't be paid.
- **Declined** — the brand rejected this reward.

The **Payout** column tells you separately whether you've actually been paid for it yet.

### On the Payouts page

Four tiles across the top: **Total Earned** and **Total Paid** for all time, plus your **Next Payout** with an estimated pay date and your **Last Payout**.

Below them, each payout carries a status:

- **Pending** — created and queued.
- **Processing** — on its way to you.
- **Paid** — the money has been sent.
- **Failed** — something went wrong, usually with your payout details. Check them in Settings and contact the brand.

The page also tells you the program's payout schedule, so you know when to expect the next one. See [Getting paid](/help-center/affiliates/payouts).

## Why your portals don't all look the same

Two things change what you see from one program to the next.

**Branding.** The brand chooses the logo, the accent color, the web address, and the languages available in the picker. Some portals are fully white-labelled to the brand's own domain.

**Features the brand turned on.** Referly gives every program the same set of tools, but brands choose which to use:

- **My Team** only appears when the program pays commission on affiliates you recruit.
- **Content Rewards**, **Performance Bonuses**, **Leaderboard**, and **Marketing Assets** are always in the sidebar, but the page tells you there's nothing available yet if the brand hasn't set anything up.
- **Promo codes** appear only when the brand has issued you one.

If a feature you want isn't there, it's the brand's decision — message their team from **Messages** and ask.

## Manage your account in Settings

**Settings** is organized into four tabs:

- **Personal Information** — your **Profile**, **Affiliate Links**, **Promo Codes**, and the **Program Questions** you answered when you applied.
- **Payouts** — **Cash Payouts** (your payment method and details), **Non-Cash Payouts**, and **Invoice Details**.
- **Preferences** — **Email Notifications** and **In-App Notifications**.
- **Advanced** — your **Postback Url**, for tracking conversions in your own tools.

See [Your profile](/help-center/affiliates/profile) and [Notifications](/help-center/affiliates/notifications).

### Change a setting everywhere at once

When you update something in your Influencer dashboard that could apply to your other programs — your name, your photo, your payout details — you'll be asked **Apply to all your programs?**

Choose **Apply to all** to push the change everywhere, or **Just this account** to keep it local. To stop being asked, open **Settings** in your Influencer dashboard and set your preference to always apply changes, never apply them, or keep asking each time.

<Note>
  This saves real time once you're in a handful of programs. Change your payout email once instead of ten times.
</Note>

## If you can't get in yet

Joining a program isn't always instant. If you land on a screen saying your application is being reviewed, the brand hasn't approved you yet — you'll be notified when they do, and the full portal unlocks then.

You may also see a message if your account was declined or deactivated by the brand. See [Access issues](/help-center/affiliates/access-issues) for what each screen means and what to do next.
