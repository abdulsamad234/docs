To promote a brand, you need an affiliate account in that brand's program. Every brand on Referly has its own affiliate portal, so you sign up separately for each one. This page walks you through signing up on a brand's portal, and what happens if you already have an account there.

Looking for new brands to promote? See [Find and join programs in the marketplace](/help-center/affiliates/join-a-program).

## Sign up on a brand's portal

### Open the brand's sign-up page

Use the sign-up link the brand shared with you. If you only have their portal address, open it and select **Sign up** under the login form.

### Enter your details

Fill in **First Name**, **Last Name** and **Email address**, then select **Sign Up**.

Use the same email address you use for your other programs. That's what keeps all your programs together in your [Influencer dashboard](/help-center/affiliates/my-programs).

![A brand's affiliate portal sign-up page with the First Name, Last Name and Email address fields, the Terms and Conditions note and the Sign Up button, next to the program's banner](/images/help-center/affiliates/affiliate-signup-form.png)

A few things you may also see on the form, depending on the brand:

- **See Commission Plan** under the form shows what the brand pays, so you can check before you join.
- **Sign up with Google** lets you sign up with your Google account instead of filling in the form.
- An **I'm not a robot** checkbox. Tick it before you select **Sign Up**.

### Enter your verification code

You'll get an email with a 6-digit code. On the **Verify Email** screen, type it in and select **Verify Email**. Once the code is accepted, your account is created and you're signed in.

Typed the wrong address? Select **Change email** to go back. If the code doesn't arrive, see [Portal access issues](/help-center/affiliates/access-issues).

## What happens after you sign up

What you see next depends on how the brand runs their program:

- Some brands ask a few questions before you reach your dashboard. See [Affiliate onboarding](/help-center/affiliates/onboarding).
- Some review every application by hand. You'll see a screen asking you to wait while they review yours, and you'll be told when you're approved.
- Otherwise you go straight to your dashboard, ready to grab your [referral link](/help-center/affiliates/referral-links).

## If you already have an account

If you sign up with an email that already has an account in this program, you won't get an error. You're emailed a sign-in code instead, and you'll see **You already have an account. We sent you a code to sign in.** You're taken straight to the login page's code screen. Enter the code from that email to sign in.

This only applies to an account with the same brand. An account with a different brand doesn't count, so you sign up as normal. If the brand invited you and you haven't signed up yet, you also go through the normal sign-up.
