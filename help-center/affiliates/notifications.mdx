Referly tells you when something happens — a click turns into a signup, a sale earns you a commission, a brand posts a new bonus. You get those in two ways: as notifications inside your portal, and as emails. You control both.

## Catch up with the bell

The bell sits in the top right of every affiliate portal. A coloured badge on it counts what you haven't read yet, and stops at **9+** rather than showing a bigger number.

{/* TODO screenshot: notification-bell — upload it in the local Documentation Agent */}

Select it to open the panel.

{/* TODO screenshot: notification-panel — upload it in the local Documentation Agent */}

Three tabs sort what's in there:

- **All** — everything, newest first.
- **Unread** — only what you haven't opened. Its own count sits in the tab. If you have anything unread when you open the bell, Referly starts you on this tab so you're not hunting.
- **Announcements** — messages the brand has broadcast to their affiliates, kept apart from your activity so a run of commission notifications doesn't bury them.

Selecting a notification takes you to the thing it's about — a new sale opens that sale, a message opens the conversation — and marks it read as you go. Scroll to the bottom of the list and older ones load automatically. If you'd rather clear the lot, **Mark all read** is in the top right of the panel; it appears only when you actually have something unread.

## See every program's notifications at once

The [Influencer dashboard](/help-center/affiliates/my-programs) has the same bell, but it pulls in notifications from every program you're part of rather than just one.

{/* TODO screenshot: master-notification-badges — upload it in the local Documentation Agent */}

Each row carries a small logo badge showing which brand it came from. Select one and Referly switches you into that program and drops you on the right page — you don't have to work out which portal to open first.

This is the fastest way to keep up once you're promoting several brands. Rather than checking five portals, you check one bell.

## Choose which emails you get

Go to **Settings**, open the **Preferences** tab, and select **Email Notifications**.

{/* TODO screenshot: preferences-tab — upload it in the local Documentation Agent */}

{/* TODO screenshot: email-notification-toggles — upload it in the local Documentation Agent */}

Each row is one kind of email, with a switch on the right. Turn a switch off and those emails stop; turn it back on and they resume. Changes save as you make them — there's no save button to remember.

Depending on the brand, you may see options like:

| Notification | When it's sent |
| --- | --- |
| **New Referral** | Someone signs up through your referral link |
| **Commission Earned** | You earn a commission from a sale |
| **Bonus Earned** | You earn a bonus |
| **Performance Bonus Announcements** | The brand adds a new performance bonus you can go after |
| **Content Reward Announcements** | The brand adds a new content reward campaign |

Below those is a separate **Message Emails** panel with a single switch, **New Chat Message**. That one sends you a digest when you've missed chat messages from the brand — so a message doesn't sit unread for days because you weren't in your portal.

<Tip>
Leave **Commission Earned** on, at least at first. It's the one email that tells you a piece of content is actually working, and it usually arrives before you'd think to check your dashboard.
</Tip>

## Choose which pop-ups you get

In the same **Preferences** tab, select **In-App Notifications**.

{/* TODO screenshot: in-app-notification-toggles — upload it in the local Documentation Agent */}

These control the small slide-in messages that appear in the corner of your portal while you're using it. They're separate from the emails, so you can have one without the other — many affiliates turn most emails off and leave the in-app ones on.

The list is usually longer than the email list, and can include a new referral, a commission earned, a bonus earned, a new performance bonus or content reward becoming available, a content reward being approved or turned down, a payout being created for you, your commission plan changing, your tier being upgraded, a new chat message, and being added to a group chat.

Turning one off only stops the pop-up. The notification still arrives in your bell, so nothing is lost — you just find it when you go looking instead of it interrupting you.

## When a notification type is missing

Each brand decides which notifications their program sends. You only see switches for the ones they've enabled, which is why the list looks different from one program to the next.

If a brand hasn't enabled any, you'll see this:

{/* TODO screenshot: no-notifications-available — upload it in the local Documentation Agent */}

**No notifications available** isn't a fault, and there's nothing to fix on your end. Your bell still works. If there's an email you'd genuinely find useful, it's worth asking that brand's affiliate manager to switch it on.

Your preferences are also per program. Turning commission emails off for one brand leaves every other brand exactly as it was.

<Tip>
Changing a setting from inside the Influencer dashboard offers to apply it across all your programs at once, so you don't have to repeat yourself ten times. See [My programs](/help-center/affiliates/my-programs).
</Tip>

## Emails have stopped arriving

Work through these in order.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Check the switch for that program">
    Open **Settings**, then **Preferences**, then **Email Notifications** in that brand's portal and confirm the switch is on. If you once used an Apply to all prompt, a change you made in one program may have been pushed to this one too.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Check your spam and promotions folders">
    Affiliate emails come from the brand's own sending address, which your email provider may not recognise at first. Find one and mark it as not spam, then add the sender to your contacts so the rest arrive properly.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Check the email address on your account">
    Emails go to the address on your affiliate profile, which may not be the one you check most. Confirm it under [your profile](/help-center/affiliates/profile).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Check the brand hasn't turned that notification off">
    If a switch you remember using has disappeared from the list entirely, the brand has disabled that notification for their whole program. Ask their affiliate manager.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
