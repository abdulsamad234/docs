A performance bonus is a one-off extra payment on top of your normal commission. The brand sets a target, and if you reach it, the bonus is yours.

It doesn't replace anything. You keep every commission you earned getting there, and the bonus lands on top.

## See what's on offer

In the left sidebar of the affiliate portal, select **Performance Bonuses**.

{/* TODO screenshot: performance-bonuses-page — upload it in the local Documentation Agent */}

Bonuses you're still working towards appear under **In Progress**. Ones you've already earned move to **Completed** below them. If the page says there are no bonuses available, the brand either hasn't set one up or hasn't opened this one to you.

## Read a bonus card

Each card tells you the whole deal in a few lines.

{/* TODO screenshot: bonus-card-anatomy — upload it in the local Documentation Agent */}

The sentence in the middle is the one that matters. It reads something like *Earn 500 USD bonus when you refer 25 customers between Mar 1st, 2026 and Apr 30th, 2026*, and it spells out the reward, the target and the window in one go.

Underneath it is your progress bar, showing where you are against the target and the same thing as a percentage. At the bottom right is what you'll get.

Select any card to open the full view, with the brand's description, the goal, the reward and the dates laid out.

## Understand what's being counted

A bonus measures one of three things, and the goal sentence names it:

| What it counts | What that means |
| --- | --- |
| **Customers referred** | How many people you've brought in |
| **Conversions or sales** | How many purchases came from your referrals |
| **Revenue** | The total value of those purchases, not your commission |

Revenue bonuses are the ones people misread. The target is the money the brand takes, not the money you take, so a 10,000 USD revenue target is reached long before you've earned 10,000 USD yourself.

## Check which activity counts

This is the other detail worth reading twice, and the wording of the goal sentence gives it away.

- **"in total"** means everything you've ever done in this program counts. If you've already passed the target, you may qualify the moment the bonus appears.
- **"after"** a date, or **"between"** two dates, means the clock starts then. Anything you referred before that date doesn't count towards this one.

So two bonuses with the same target can be very different amounts of work. Check the sentence before you plan around it.

When a bonus has no dates on the card, there's no deadline. When it shows a range, you have until the end date.

## Watch your progress

Your progress bar updates as your referrals and sales come in, so you don't have to do anything to keep it current. Come back to the page whenever you want to see where you are.

A couple of things to expect:

- **Progress follows approved activity.** A sale that's still being processed or that gets refunded won't hold its place on the bar.
- **Progress is per program.** Only what you've done in this program counts towards its bonuses.

For the underlying numbers, open **Referrals** and **Rewards** in the left sidebar.

## Collect the bonus

When you cross the target, the bonus is awarded to you without you having to claim it. The card moves to **Completed**, picks up a green pill, and shows the date you earned it.

Open it and you'll see a **Bonus earned** banner with the same date.

{/* TODO screenshot: bonus-earned-badge — upload it in the local Documentation Agent */}

The amount then shows up in **Rewards** alongside your commissions, and goes out with your next payout on the program's normal schedule. See [how you get paid](/help-center/affiliates/payouts).

<Note>
  Each bonus can be earned once. Passing the target again doesn't pay it twice. Brands who want a repeatable incentive usually run a series of bonuses with rising targets instead.
</Note>

If a brand launches a bonus you already qualify for, they can award it to you straight away rather than making you start over. In that case it appears in **Completed** the first time you see it.

## Why another affiliate sees different bonuses

Brands can offer a bonus to everyone, to particular [affiliate groups](/help-center/manage/affiliates/groups), or to named individuals. A top-tier partner may have a bonus in their portal that isn't in yours, and one built for a single partner won't appear for anyone else.

If you heard about a bonus you can't find, that's usually the reason. Ask your program manager whether you're included.

## Get told when a new bonus appears

You can be notified when a brand launches one. In **Settings**, open the **Preferences** tab, and you'll find **Performance Bonus Announcements** under email notifications and **New Bonus Available** under in-app notifications.

{/* TODO screenshot: bonus-notification-settings — upload it in the local Documentation Agent */}

Worth leaving on. Bonuses with a short window are easy to miss otherwise. More in [your notifications](/help-center/affiliates/notifications).

## Bonuses and milestones aren't the same thing

Both reward you for hitting numbers, but what you get is different.

| | Performance bonus | Milestone |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What you get | A one-off payment | A better commission plan |
| How long it lasts | Paid once | Applies to everything you earn from then on |
| Where you see it | The **Performance Bonuses** page | Your commission plan |

To see your milestones, open **My Commission Plan** from **Quick Links** on your dashboard, then **View All Milestones**. It shows every milestone, what each one needs, and the plan it upgrades you to.

{/* TODO screenshot: milestones-dialog — upload it in the local Documentation Agent */}

A milestone is usually the bigger prize over time. A bonus is the faster one.

## If you promote several programs

Bonuses belong to the program that created them, so there's no combined view. From the Influencer dashboard at [influencers.referly.so](https://influencers.referly.so), open **My Programs** and select **Go to program**, then **Performance Bonuses** in that portal's sidebar. See [your programs](/help-center/affiliates/my-programs).
