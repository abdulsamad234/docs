The first time you enter a program's affiliate portal after joining, you're taken through a short guided setup. It collects the things the brand needs before they can approve you and pay you — who you are, where you promote, and how the money should reach you.

It takes a couple of minutes, and everything in it can be changed later.

## What to expect

Onboarding is a series of steps with a **Step 1 of 4** style counter at the top, so you always know how much is left. At the bottom you'll find **Save and continue**, **Back**, and — on steps the brand allows you to pass — **Skip for now**.

Each brand decides which steps to include, what order they come in, and whether any of them can be skipped. That means the flow you get from one program won't be identical to the next. A few brands turn onboarding off entirely and drop you straight into the portal.

**Summary** is always the last step.

![onboarding-flow](https://qnthgdfusotvjgaxpfie.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/public-assets/docs/uploads/1784819074916-637787-final-onboarding-flow-1784819074387.png)

<Note>
  Some programs let you leave the whole flow with **Skip onboarding**. It's there for when you're in a hurry — but anything you skip, the brand doesn't have, and payout details in particular have to exist before you can be paid.
</Note>

## Basic information

The first step asks who you are.

- **First name** and **Last name** are required. Use the name you want the brand to see, and the name that should appear on payouts.
- **Profile photo** is optional. Select the photo area to upload one. It needs to be a PNG, JPEG, or WebP image under 1MB.

A photo isn't cosmetic when a brand is reviewing applications by hand — a real name and a real face get approved more often than a blank profile.

![profile-step](https://qnthgdfusotvjgaxpfie.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/public-assets/docs/uploads/1784818376115-269178-final-profile-step-1784818375999.png)

### Choosing your categories

In your Influencer dashboard at `influencers.referly.so`, this step is called **Basic information** and adds one more thing: **Categories**, the niches you create content in. You can pick up to three, and at least one is required.

This is worth taking seriously. Your categories feed the match score you see on every marketplace listing — the percentage that tells you how well a program fits you. Programs in your niches score higher, and the **Recommended for you** row on the marketplace home is built from them. See [Join a program](/help-center/affiliates/join-a-program).

You can change your categories any time from **Settings** in your Influencer dashboard.

![categories-picker](https://qnthgdfusotvjgaxpfie.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/public-assets/docs/uploads/1784818372320-21981-final-categories-picker-1784818372152.png)

## Website and socials

The **Website and socials** step is where you add the places you'll actually promote from: your **Website**, **YouTube**, **X (Twitter)**, **Instagram**, **TikTok**, and **LinkedIn**.

Enter your handle or address for each one. Brands choose which platforms to ask about, and can mark some as **Required** — those show the word next to the field.

Next to each account you'll see a **Verify** button. Verifying proves the account is yours and shows the brand your real follower or subscriber count. Verified accounts sit next to your name when a brand reviews you, which makes a visible difference to whether you get accepted — especially on programs that approve affiliates by hand.

Verification takes an extra minute per platform and each one works a little differently. See [Connect and verify your social accounts](/help-center/affiliates/social-accounts) for the walkthrough.

![socials-step](https://qnthgdfusotvjgaxpfie.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/public-assets/docs/uploads/1784818376840-128993-final-socials-step-1784818376656.png)

## Payout settings

**Payout settings** is how you get paid. Pick a **Payout method** from the ones this brand supports:

- **PayPal** — payments to your PayPal account.
- **Wise** — fast international transfers through Wise.
- **Local Bank Transfer** — straight into your local bank account. You choose the **Currency** you want to be paid in, then fill in the bank fields for that currency, which change by country.
- **Bank Transfer** — a direct bank deposit.
- **Credits** — your commissions are added to your account balance with the brand instead of being paid out in cash.

For the email-based methods you also enter a **Payout email**. Check it carefully — this is where the money goes, and a typo means a failed payout.

Not every program offers every method, so this list looks different from program to program. See [Cash payouts](/help-center/affiliates/cash-payouts) and [Non-cash payouts](/help-center/affiliates/non-cash-payouts).

![payout-step](https://qnthgdfusotvjgaxpfie.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/public-assets/docs/uploads/1784818374465-770501-final-payout-step-1784818374346.png)

<Warning>
  You can skip this step if the brand allows it, but you won't be paid until it's filled in. If you skip, come back to it in **Settings** before your first payout is due.
</Warning>

## Invoice information

**Invoice information** is the billing detail that goes on your payout paperwork.

Start by choosing your **Entity type**:

- **Individual** — you're paid as a person. You'll be asked for your **Full name**, address, and an optional **Tax ID**.
- **Business** — you're paid as a company. The name field becomes **Company name**, and you're asked for a **VAT / Tax ID**.

Then fill in your **Invoice email**, **Address**, **City**, **State**, **Zip code**, and **Country**.

If you enter an EU **VAT Number**, it's checked against the EU VAT registry as you type, and a verified badge appears once it clears. A number that doesn't validate usually means a wrong country prefix or a mistyped digit.

Getting this right up front saves back-and-forth later, because it's what appears on the invoices generated for your payouts. See [Invoice details](/help-center/affiliates/invoice-details).

![invoice-step](https://qnthgdfusotvjgaxpfie.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/public-assets/docs/uploads/1784818373733-943722-final-invoice-step-1784818373515.png)

## The brand's own questions

Many programs add their own steps with questions they want answered before approving you — which platforms you post on, how big your audience is, why you want to join, sometimes a file upload for a media kit or ID.

Question types vary: short answers, longer written answers, dropdowns, multiple choice, and file uploads. Required questions have to be answered before you can continue; the rest you can leave blank.

Answer these properly. On programs that review applications by hand, this is the main thing the brand reads when deciding. "I'll post about it" and a link to a real channel with real numbers are not treated the same way.

Some answers can be edited later from **Settings**, and some are locked once submitted — that's the brand's choice per question.

![custom-questions-step](https://qnthgdfusotvjgaxpfie.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/public-assets/docs/uploads/1784818372944-722298-final-custom-questions-step-1784818372858.png)

## Summary

The last step confirms you're done, and what you see depends on whether the brand approves affiliates automatically.

**If you're approved automatically**, you'll see **You're all set!** along with:

- A summary of the **Rewards** you can earn in this program, with **View commission plan** for the full detail.
- **Your referral links**, ready to copy.
- **Your promo codes**, if the brand issues them.

Select **Go to dashboard** to enter the portal and start promoting.

**If the brand reviews applications by hand**, you'll see **Application submitted** instead. Your details are with the brand, and you'll be notified once they approve you. The full portal unlocks then. See [Access issues](/help-center/affiliates/access-issues) if you're unsure what a waiting screen means.

![summary-step](https://qnthgdfusotvjgaxpfie.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/public-assets/docs/uploads/1784819076780-747142-final-summary-step-1784819075865.png)

## Change anything later

Nothing you enter during onboarding is permanent. In the portal, open **Settings** in the left sidebar and you'll find the same information under four tabs:

- **Personal Information** — your profile, affiliate links, promo codes, and the program's questions.
- **Payouts** — cash payouts, non-cash payouts, and invoice details.
- **Preferences** — email and in-app notifications.
- **Advanced** — your postback URL.

See [Your profile](/help-center/affiliates/profile).

### Update every program at once

Filling in the same payout email for ten programs is a waste of your time, so you don't have to.

When you change something in your Influencer dashboard that could apply elsewhere — your name, photo, payout details, invoice details, categories, or social accounts — you're asked **Apply to all your programs?** Choose **Apply to all** and the change lands everywhere at once.

You can set this to happen automatically, or to never happen, from **Settings** in your Influencer dashboard.

<Tip>
  Do your onboarding in the Influencer dashboard first, then join programs. The details you've already entered are copied into each new program you join, so most of the flow is pre-filled by the time you get there.
</Tip>
