Add-ons switch on extra sets of features on top of your plan. There are three: **Engage** for talking to your affiliates, **Gamify** for contests and rewards, and **Influencer Discovery** for recruiting creators from Referly's own network. Some plans include them, and on other plans you can add the ones you need and pay for them monthly or yearly.

You'll find all three in one place: open **Settings** from the left sidebar, go to **Billing**, select **Subscription**, and scroll to the **Add-ons** card.

![The Add-ons card on the Subscription screen listing the Engagement, Gamification and Influencer Discovery add-ons with Manage add-on and Buy add-on buttons](/images/help-center/billing/addons-card.png)

## The add-ons and their prices

Prices are in US dollars. Each add-on is its own subscription, billed separately from your plan.

| Add-on | Monthly | Yearly |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Engage | $67 | $673 |
| Gamify | $67 | $673 |
| Influencer Discovery | $199 | $1,990 |

<Note>
  Your billing page lists Engage as **Engagement add-on** and Gamify as **Gamification add-on**. They're the same add-ons.
</Note>

## What each add-on unlocks

### Engage

Engage turns on the tools for keeping your affiliates active and informed:

- Unlimited [messaging](/help-center/engage/messages/overview), including [group chats and broadcasts](/help-center/engage/messages/groups)
- Full chat history and file attachments, and [sharing marketing assets and performance bonuses in chat](/help-center/engage/messages/share-assets-and-bonuses)
- Unlimited [announcements](/help-center/engage/announcements/overview), with automations that send them for you
- [Affiliate acquisition](/help-center/manage/analytics/affiliate-acquisition) and onboarding funnel reports

### Gamify

Gamify adds ways to reward your best affiliates and keep everyone competing:

- [Contests](/help-center/gamify/contests/overview) with cash and non-cash prizes
- Unlimited live [performance bonuses](/help-center/gamify/performance-bonuses/overview), however many your plan normally allows
- [Affiliate milestones](/help-center/gamify/milestones/overview) with automatic tier upgrades (also included with Business and higher plans)
- [Content rewards](/help-center/gamify/content-rewards/overview) for creator posts
- The option to hide your [public leaderboard](/help-center/gamify/leaderboards/public)

### Influencer Discovery

This add-on opens [Influencer Discovery](/help-center/grow/influencer-discovery/overview), in the **Grow** section of your sidebar, so you can recruit creators who already promote other programs:

- Search Referly's own network of affiliates and influencers
- See real revenue and conversion data on each profile
- Send up to 500 [invitations](/help-center/grow/influencer-discovery/invite) a month
- Offer signing bonuses that pay out when a creator accepts

<Info>
  Influencer Discovery needs an active paid plan. You can't add it during a free trial or without a plan. Start your paid plan first, then come back to add it.
</Info>

## Which plans include add-ons

| Plan | Add-ons included |
| --- | --- |
| Startup, Business, Agency | None. Add the ones you need. |
| Agency Pro | One of your choice |
| Enterprise and Agency Scale | All three, with 500 Influencer Discovery invitations a month |

When an add-on comes with your plan, its row on the **Add-ons** card says **Included with Enterprise** or **Included with your plan**, and there's nothing to buy. See [plans and features](/help-center/billing/plans-and-features) for everything else each plan includes.

### Pick your included add-on on Agency Pro

Agency Pro includes one add-on at no extra cost, and you choose which one.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your subscription">
    Go to **Settings**, then **Billing**, then **Subscription**. While you haven't chosen yet, a **Pick your included add-on** card appears above the **Add-ons** card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your add-on">
    Select **Choose** next to **Engage**, **Gamify**, or **Influencer Discovery**. The add-on switches on straight away, and its row on the **Add-ons** card shows an **Included with Agency Pro** badge.
  </Step>
</Steps>

![The Pick your included add-on card on an Agency Pro subscription, with Engage, Gamify and Influencer Discovery each offering a Choose button](/images/help-center/billing/pick-included-addon.png)

You can also make your choice from an upgrade prompt: when you open a feature that one of the add-ons unlocks, the prompt shows **Use your included add-on**.

<Warning>
  The choice is one-time. Pick the add-on your team will use most. If you need to change it later, contact support.
</Warning>

## Buy an add-on

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Add-ons card">
    Go to **Settings**, then **Billing**, then **Subscription**, and scroll to **Add-ons**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Buy add-on">
    Select **Buy add-on** on the add-on's row. A window opens listing what the add-on includes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose monthly or yearly">
    Turn on the **Billed annually** switch to pay yearly instead of monthly. The button updates to show the price, for example **Add for $67/mo**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pay at checkout">
    Select the button and complete payment on the secure checkout page. When you're done you're brought back to Referly, and the add-on's features unlock.
  </Step>
</Steps>

![The Influencer Discovery add-on dialog with what is included, the Billed annually switch, the yearly price and the Add button](/images/help-center/billing/buy-addon-dialog.png)

You don't have to go to the billing page first. Whenever you open a feature that an add-on unlocks, the upgrade prompt names the add-on and has the same **Add for** button.

## Manage an add-on you pay for

Each row on the **Add-ons** card shows its status:

- **Not subscribed**: you don't have it yet. Select **Buy add-on** to add it.
- **Subscribed monthly** or **Subscribed yearly**: you're paying for it. Select **Manage add-on** to open the billing page for that add-on's subscription.
- **Included with Enterprise** or **Included with your plan**: it comes with your plan, so there's no button.
