Every time Referly bills you for your plan, an invoice is created. You can find them all in your dashboard, open any one in full, and pay one that's outstanding.

## Find your invoices

Open **Settings** from the left sidebar, go to **Billing**, then select **Invoices**. You can also open [search](/help-center/getting-started/global-search) and type "invoices" to jump straight there.

The **Invoices** screen lists your invoices, one row each. Every row shows:

- **The amount** of the invoice.
- **A status badge**: **Paid**, **Unpaid**, or **Canceled**.
- **What it's for**, a short description of what you were billed for, such as your plan.
- **The date** the invoice was created and its **invoice number**.

Only invoices for more than $0 are listed. If you haven't been billed yet, the screen says "No invoices found".

<Note>
  Add-ons are billed separately from your plan, so their invoices don't appear in this list. To manage an add-on's billing, select **Manage add-on** next to it in the **Add-ons** card on the [Subscription](/help-center/billing/subscription) screen. This opens that add-on's billing in Stripe's secure portal.
</Note>

## View or download an invoice

Select **View** on any invoice. It opens on Stripe's secure invoice page in a new tab, where you can see the full breakdown and download a copy for your records.

## Pay an unpaid invoice

An invoice that hasn't been paid shows an **Unpaid** badge and a **Pay Now** button. Select **Pay Now** to open the invoice on Stripe's secure page and pay it there.

You'll also be reminded in two other places while an invoice is outstanding:

- A red banner across the top of your dashboard reads "Unpaid Invoice" with the amount, and has its own **Pay Now** button.
- On the [Subscription](/help-center/billing/subscription) screen, an **Overdue** badge appears next to your **Next payment** date, with a **Pay now** button underneath.

<Warning>
  Pay outstanding invoices promptly. The dashboard banner warns that your affiliate programs may be deactivated soon if an invoice stays unpaid.
</Warning>

## Invoices for agencies and their clients

- **If you run an agency**, your agency's own Referly invoices are in your agency settings: open **Billing**, then **Invoices**. They work the same way as described above.
- **If an agency runs your program** and bills you through Referly, the **Invoices** screen inside that program shows the invoices your agency has sent you for it.

See [billing for agencies](/help-center/agencies/billing) for how agency billing works.
