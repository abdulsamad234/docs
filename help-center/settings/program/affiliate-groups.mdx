Not every affiliate deserves the same deal. The creator who sends you fifty customers a month shouldn't be on the same rate as someone who signed up last week, and the agency you partner with probably needs its own landing pages and promo codes. Affiliate groups are how you draw those lines: you create a group, and that group can carry its own commission plans, landing pages, and promo codes.

To find them, go to **Settings**, open **Program details**, and select **Affiliate groups**.

## How grouping works

Every affiliate in your program belongs somewhere. Anyone you haven't placed in a group sits in a built-in group called **Default**, described on screen as "Affiliates not assigned to any group are in default group". You never create or manage this one — it's simply where everyone starts.

That makes grouping additive rather than something you have to plan up front. You can run a program for months with everyone in **Default**, then create a **Top Performers** group the day you're ready to reward your best affiliates differently.

Above the list you'll see a counter reading **Groups:** followed by how many you've created. Most paid accounts show **unlimited**; accounts on an AppSumo plan are capped at two groups and show a **Limit reached** badge once they get there. Affiliate groups are a Business-plan feature, so on lower plans the create button prompts you to upgrade instead.

## Create a group

Select **Create group** at the top right. If you haven't made one yet, there's also a **Create your first affiliate group** button filling the empty space where the list will go.

{/* TODO screenshot: create-group-dialog — upload it in the local Documentation Agent */}

The dialog asks for two things:

- **Group name** — what you'll call it, with a hint under the field reading "For internal use only, never shown to affiliates". That's worth taking literally: name it for your own clarity. **High-volume EU partners** is a perfectly good group name precisely because no affiliate will ever read it.
- **Color** — one of twelve preset swatches. The color becomes the accent bar and dot on the group's card, which is the fastest way to tell groups apart at a glance. There's no custom color entry.

Select **Create group** to finish, and you'll see **Affiliate group created**. Leave the name blank and you'll be told **Group name is required**.

## Read a group's card

Each group gets a card showing what's happening inside it.

{/* TODO screenshot: group-card-stats — upload it in the local Documentation Agent */}

Under the group name, a line tells you how much is configured for it — for example "1 commission plan" and "3 tiers". Below that are five figures:

- **Affiliates** — how many people are in the group
- **Clicks** — clicks on their links
- **Referrals** — referrals they've generated
- **Gross Revenue** — revenue before deductions
- **Net Revenue** — what's left after them

**Affiliates**, **Referrals**, and **Gross Revenue** are clickable. Selecting one opens your Affiliates, Referrals, or Transactions list already filtered to that group, which saves you rebuilding the same filter every time you want to check on a segment.

## Invite affiliates straight into a group

Each group card has a link icon with the tooltip **Copy sign-up link**. Selecting it copies a sign-up link tied to that specific group and confirms with **Sign-up link copied to clipboard**.

This is the neatest way to fill a group. Send that link to the ten creators you recruited at a conference and they land in your **Creators** group when they join — no sorting afterwards. Use your normal sign-up link for everyone else and they arrive in **Default** as usual.

For affiliates who have already joined, you change their group from your **Affiliates** list rather than from this page.

## Edit or delete a group

The three-dot menu on a group card holds **Edit** and **Delete**.

{/* TODO screenshot: group-actions-menu — upload it in the local Documentation Agent */}

**Edit** reopens the same dialog so you can change the group's **Group name** and **Color** — and only those two. A group's commission plans, landing pages, and promo codes are set up on their own pages and pointed at the group from there, so you won't find them in this dialog.

**Delete** is deliberately hard to reach. It only becomes available once a group is empty; while anyone is still in it, the option is greyed out with a tooltip telling you to remove the affiliates first, such as "Remove all 5 affiliates from this group before deleting". Move those affiliates to another group from your **Affiliates** list, then come back.

<Warning>
Deleting a group also deletes the commission plans and tiers that belong to it, and it can't be undone. The confirmation spells this out: "Any commission plans and tiers belonging to this group will also be removed." If those terms are still in use, move the affiliates somewhere with equivalent terms before you delete.
</Warning>

## What to do with a group once you have one

A group on its own doesn't change anything — it's a label until you attach terms to it. The three things worth attaching:

- **A commission plan.** Give the group its own rate so your best affiliates earn more without you editing accounts one at a time. See [Commission plans](/help-center/getting-started/commissions/overview).
- **Landing pages.** Restrict a landing page to a group so, say, only your agency partners can send traffic to a partner-specific page. You set this up in [General program settings](/help-center/settings/program/general).
- **Promo codes.** Give a group its own discount codes to share.
