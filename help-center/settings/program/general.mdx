Almost everything that defines your affiliate program lives on one page: what it's called, where affiliates sign in, where their links send people, and — the part that matters most to them — how much they earn. This is the page you fill in when you launch a program, and the one you come back to when you want to change the deal.

To get there, go to **Settings**, open **Program details**, and select **General**.

{/* TODO screenshot: general-page-overview — upload it in the local Documentation Agent */}

One habit to learn before you start: a **Save Changes** button appears at the bottom right of the screen only once you've actually changed something. If you don't see it, nothing is waiting to be saved. Two things on this page ignore that button and save the moment you touch them — the auto-approve settings and the tax and shipping deduction toggles — and both are called out below.

## Name your program

**Affiliate Program Name** is what your affiliates see, and what identifies this program in your dashboard. Use the name people already associate with your brand rather than an internal codename — affiliates read it in their portal and in emails from you.

The name can't be left empty. If you clear it and try to save, you'll be told **Campaign name is required**.

## Set your affiliate portal address

**Affiliate Portal Subdomain** is the web address where your affiliates sign in and manage their links. You choose the first part; Referly adds the rest, so a subdomain of `acme` gives your affiliates `acme.referly.so`. If you're on an agency account with your own verified domain, the suffix shows that domain instead.

{/* TODO screenshot: subdomain-availability — upload it in the local Documentation Agent */}

As you type, Referly checks whether the address is free. A moment after you stop typing, you'll see either a green check mark (it's yours) or a red X with a short explanation (someone already has it). Anything you type is lowercased automatically, since web addresses aren't case sensitive.

Pick this carefully. It's the address you'll put in recruitment emails and on your affiliate landing page, so changing it later means every old link stops working.

## Choose where your affiliate links send people

The **Landing Page URLs** section sets the destination of every affiliate link in your program. When an affiliate shares their link, this is the page the visitor actually lands on — usually your homepage, a product page, or a campaign-specific page.

{/* TODO screenshot: landing-page-urls — upload it in the local Documentation Agent */}

You can add more than one landing page, which is useful when different affiliates promote different things. With multiple pages you can also control who sees which one, restricting a page to particular affiliates or groups. Two rules apply when you save:

- You need at least one landing page. Saving without one gives you **At least one landing page URL is required**.
- If you have several and you've restricted them all, saving fails with **At least one landing page must be available to all affiliates. Please remove affiliate restrictions from at least one landing page.** Leave one page unrestricted as the fallback for everyone. (With only a single landing page this is handled for you — it's automatically available to all affiliates.)

## Set your base commission plan

The **Base Commission Plan** card, marked with a **Default Plan** badge, is the deal every affiliate gets unless you've put them on a plan of their own. Everything from here down describes how affiliates earn.

### Choose how you reward affiliates

**Reward Type** offers two paths:

- **Cash Reward** — pay affiliates with cash commissions. This is the default and what most programs use.
- **Non-Cash Rewards** — reward affiliates with coupons or credits instead of money.

Your choice here changes every field that follows, so decide it first. The cash path is covered next; skip ahead to [Reward affiliates with coupons or credits](#reward-affiliates-with-coupons-or-credits) if you're paying in something other than money.

### Decide what earns a commission

With **Cash Reward** selected, choose when affiliates get paid:

- **Customer Makes a Payment** — pay commission when a referred customer completes a purchase. This is the default and the right choice for most stores and subscription products.
- **New Lead Signs Up** — pay a flat rate when a referred person creates an account, whether or not they ever buy.

Choosing **New Lead Signs Up** simplifies the rest of the card: you're paying a fixed amount per sign-up, so the percentage and recurring options disappear.

### Pay once or pay on every payment

For purchase-based commissions, **Commission frequency** decides how long an affiliate keeps earning from a customer they sent you:

- **First Payment Only** — commission on the customer's first purchase and nothing after.
- **Recurring Basis** — commission on multiple payments from the same customer. This is what makes subscription programs attractive to affiliates.

### Limit commission to certain products

By default, affiliates earn on everything a referred customer buys. If you'd rather they only earn on specific items, search for them under **Customer buys any of these products/plans:** and select them. Leave it blank and commission applies to all purchases. If you sell on Shopify, you can select whole collections here, not just individual products.

This restriction is all-or-nothing for the base plan — you can't set one rate for one product and a different rate for another. Referly tells you as much with a note steering you to create a separate commission plan for that. See [Commission plans](/help-center/getting-started/commissions/overview).

### Set the rate

**Commission Type** picks the shape of the payment:

- **Percentage** — a share of each sale.
- **Flat Rate** — a fixed amount per sale.

Then enter the number in **Commission Amount**. The field shows a `%` or your program's currency symbol so it's clear which you're entering. Percentage rates can't be negative or above 100 — both are caught when you save, with **Percentage commission rate cannot exceed 100%.** or **Please enter a valid commission rate that is not negative.**

### Decide how long recurring commission lasts

If you chose **Recurring Basis**, two dropdowns cap how long an affiliate keeps earning from one customer:

- **Give Commission For How Many Payments?** — up to a set number of payments, or **No Limit**.
- **Give Commission For How Many Months?** — up to a set number of months, or **Forever**.

{/* TODO screenshot: recurring-limits — upload it in the local Documentation Agent */}

The label between them, **Whichever comes first**, is the important part: both limits run at the same time and commission stops as soon as either one is reached. Twelve payments and six months means an affiliate stops earning at six months even if the customer has only paid six times. Set one to unlimited if you only want the other to apply.

### Step the rate down over time

Plenty of programs pay generously at first and less later. Select **Add commission after period** to add a follow-on rate, shown beneath a **THEN** divider. Each one has its own **Commission Rate**, **For How Many Payments?**, and **For How Many Months?**, so you can build a ladder — say 30% for the first year, then 15% for as long as the customer stays.

{/* TODO screenshot: then-milestones — upload it in the local Documentation Agent */}

Two things to know. A milestone set to unlimited (**No Limit** and **Forever**) ends the ladder, because nothing can come after "forever" — so make your final step the unlimited one. And you can remove any milestone with the trash icon beside it.

## Reward affiliates with coupons or credits

Choosing **Non-Cash Rewards** lets you pay affiliates in something other than money. **Non-Cash Reward Type** offers:

- **Coupon** — reward affiliates with discount coupons they can use or share.
- **Custom Credits** — reward affiliates with store credits or your own unit, like coins or AI credits.

Either way you then pick the trigger — **When Customer Signs Up** or **When Customer Purchases** — and the settings adapt to your choice.

{/* TODO screenshot: non-cash-reward-types — upload it in the local Documentation Agent */}

For coupons, you tell Referly where the coupon should be created: **API** if you generate codes on your own server, or **Shopify**, **Stripe**, or **WooCommerce** to create them in a connected store. The detailed coupon fields only appear once that source is ready, so if you see a **Connect** prompt instead of settings, connect the store first. From there you set a **Discount Type** (**Percentage** or **Fixed Amount**), the amount off, and — under **Advanced Coupon Settings** — how long the discount lasts (**Forever**, **Multiple Months**, or **One-time**). You can also cap how many coupons one affiliate ever earns.

For credits, **Credit Type** decides how they're fulfilled: **Shopify Store Credits**, **Stripe Credits**, **WooCommerce Credits**, or **Custom** if you handle distribution yourself. Custom credits need a **Credit unit name** — whatever you call them to affiliates. You can award a fixed number of credits per purchase, or scale them to the purchase amount, and cap how many times an affiliate can earn.

Non-cash rewards are a paid feature, so the option may appear locked depending on your plan. There's a fuller walkthrough in [Non-cash rewards](/help-center/getting-started/commissions/non-cash-rewards).

## Decide what gets approved automatically

At the bottom of the commission card, select **Auto-approve settings** to open a small dialog with three switches, all on by default:

- **Auto approve affiliates** — new affiliates are approved the moment they apply and can start promoting right away. Turn it off if you want to vet people first; you'll then approve each one by hand.
- **Auto approve rewards** — earnings are accepted as they come in. Turn it off and rewards go on hold until you approve them. Worth knowing before you flip it: sales left on hold are not paid out.
- **Apply to all commission plans** — pushes these two choices to every commission plan you have now and every one you create later.

{/* TODO screenshot: auto-approve-dialog — upload it in the local Documentation Agent */}

This dialog has its own **Save** button and saves on its own, separately from the rest of the page. You'll see **Auto-approve settings saved** when it works.

## Advanced settings

Open the **Advanced Settings** section for the details that rarely change once you've set them.

{/* TODO screenshot: advanced-settings — upload it in the local Documentation Agent */}

### The tracking parameter in your links

**Choose how affiliates will be tracked in your URLs** sets the little tag Referly adds to a link to identify the affiliate. It's `ref` unless you change it, producing links like `yoursite.com/?ref=jane`. Some teams change it to match what they already use elsewhere. It can't be blank — you'll get **URL tracking parameter is required** on save.

### Let affiliates manage their own links

**Allow affiliates to create and manage their own referral links** lets affiliates create extra links with their own custom tags, instead of using only the one you gave them. It's off by default and available on the Business plan.

Turning it on or off applies to every existing affiliate immediately, not just new ones. Their links still point at the landing pages you configured above — affiliates choose their tag, not the destination.

### Campaign Currency

**Campaign Currency** is the currency for every commission and payout in this program. It's US Dollar unless you change it, and the list shows each option as a code and name together, like `USD - US Dollar`. Set it before you have real earnings on the books, since it's the unit everything is calculated and paid in.

### Deduct tax and shipping before calculating commission

Two switches decide what counts as the revenue a commission is based on, and both are on by default:

- **Automatically deduct tax from commission calculation**
- **Automatically deduct shipping from commission calculation**

With both on, an affiliate earns on the value of the goods rather than on tax and delivery charges you never really made margin on. Turn one off and that amount goes back into the figure commission is calculated from.

These two save the instant you toggle them — you'll see **Commission deduction settings saved** — so there's no need to press **Save Changes** afterwards.

## Save your work

When you're done, select **Save Changes** at the bottom right. A confirmation reads **Program details saved successfully**. If the button is missing, you haven't changed anything; if it won't respond, check that your subdomain has finished its availability check and shows a green check mark.
