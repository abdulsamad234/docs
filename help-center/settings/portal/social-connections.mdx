Some of your best affiliates are creators with real audiences — a YouTube channel, an engaged Instagram following, a niche newsletter. Referly can ask affiliates to connect those accounts when they join, so you can see who actually has reach before you invest time in them. Once an affiliate connects a platform, you get their follower and subscriber counts, and for many platforms their audience demographics and geography too.

You control the whole thing from one place: the social media step in your onboarding. This page walks through turning that step on, choosing which platforms affiliates can connect, deciding what's required, and then — the payoff — filtering and sorting your affiliate list by platform and audience size, and reading each affiliate's audience data.

## Turn on the social media step

The request to connect social accounts is one step in your affiliate onboarding. To find it, go to **Settings**, open **Affiliate portal**, and select **Onboarding**. You'll see the list of steps a new affiliate goes through, including one called **Website & Socials**.

![social-step-settings](https://qnthgdfusotvjgaxpfie.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/public-assets/docs/uploads/1784643750252-256262-SCR-20260721-otuu.jpeg)

Each step has an eye icon to show or hide it. If **Website & Socials** shows a **Hidden** badge, select the eye icon to make it visible — affiliates won't be asked to connect anything until the step is on. Select the step to expand it and reveal its options.

Two settings apply to the step as a whole:

- **Title** and **Subtitle** — the heading and short line affiliates read at this step. The defaults ("Website & Socials") work well, but you can reword them to fit your program.
- **Skippable** — when on, affiliates can move past this step without connecting anything. Turn it off if connecting an account should be part of finishing onboarding. Keep in mind that requiring a connection adds friction, so weigh how much you need the data against how many sign-ups you want to complete.

## Choose which platforms affiliates can connect

Inside the step, **Social Channels** lists every platform an affiliate can add:

- **Website** — the affiliate's personal site or blog
- **YouTube** — channel with subscriber count
- **X / Twitter** — profile with follower count
- **Instagram** — profile with follower count
- **TikTok** — profile with follower count
- **LinkedIn** — professional profile

Each platform has its own on/off toggle. Turn off any platform that isn't relevant to your program — if your affiliates are bloggers, you might keep Website and YouTube and hide the rest, so affiliates aren't asked for accounts they don't have.

At least one platform has to stay on while the step is visible, so you'll always be collecting something. If you don't want to ask for social accounts at all, hide the whole step instead.

## Decide what's required

How strict the step is comes down to one toggle at the top: **Require at least one social channel**.

![require-social-toggles](https://qnthgdfusotvjgaxpfie.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/public-assets/docs/uploads/1784643757576-163562-SCR-20260721-ouii.png)

### Ask for at least one account (the simple option)

With **Require at least one social channel** turned **on**, affiliates must connect at least one of your enabled platforms before they can finish onboarding, but they choose which one. This is the most common setup: you guarantee every affiliate hands over some audience data without forcing a specific network on someone who isn't on it.

When this is on, a single **Require verification** toggle appears beneath it. Turn it on to require that the account an affiliate connects is verified — that is, that they've proven they own it, not just typed in a handle. See [What verification means](#what-verification-means) below.

### Require specific platforms (the granular option)

Turn **Require at least one social channel** **off** and each enabled platform gains its own two toggles:

![per-channel-required](https://qnthgdfusotvjgaxpfie.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/public-assets/docs/uploads/1784643771654-539514-SCR-20260721-ourr.png)

- **Required** — the affiliate must add this specific platform to continue. Use it when a particular network is essential to your program (for example, requiring Instagram for a beauty brand).
- **Require verification** — the affiliate must verify ownership of that platform, not just enter a handle. This becomes available once **Required** is on for that platform.

Leave a platform's **Required** toggle off and it stays optional — affiliates can connect it if they want to, but they're not blocked if they skip it.

### What verification means

Anyone can type a username into a form. **Verification** is how Referly confirms an affiliate genuinely owns the account they connected, so the follower counts and audience data you're looking at are real.

For most platforms, the affiliate verifies by signing in to that account and approving access — a quick, standard sign-in. A personal Instagram account is verified a little differently, by adding a short code to the profile bio. Either way, once an account is verified it earns a verified badge everywhere it appears in your dashboard, and only verified accounts show full audience insights.

Requiring verification raises the quality of your data, but it also asks more of the affiliate. If you mainly want a rough sense of reach, asking for a connection without verification is lighter; if you're making decisions based on the numbers, require verification.

## How affiliates connect and verify

Once the step is on, affiliates connect their accounts during onboarding, and can always add or update them later from their portal under **Settings** → **Personal Info** → **Website & Socials**.

![affiliate-connect-socials](https://qnthgdfusotvjgaxpfie.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/public-assets/docs/uploads/1784643799661-866713-Connect-youtube.gif)

## Filter and sort affiliates by platform and audience size

The reason to collect this data is to act on it. On your **Affiliates** list, connected accounts show up in the **Social Channels** column as platform chips with follower counts, and two filters let you cut the list down to exactly the affiliates you're looking for.

Select **Filter** above the list to open the filter menu.

### Filter by platform

Choose **Social Platforms** to narrow the list to affiliates who've connected a particular network. Pick TikTok to see only your TikTok creators, for instance. Hold **Shift** while selecting to choose more than one platform at once.

![filter-social-platforms](https://qnthgdfusotvjgaxpfie.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/public-assets/docs/uploads/1784643921795-856544-SCR-20260721-owex.png)

### Filter by audience size

Choose **Audience Size** to filter by how large an affiliate's following is. First pick a platform — **All** to add up an affiliate's reach across every connected account, or a single platform to measure just that one — then set a **Min** and **Max** follower or subscriber count. Setting a minimum of 10,000 on Instagram, for example, surfaces only your larger Instagram creators.

![filter-audience-size](https://qnthgdfusotvjgaxpfie.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/public-assets/docs/uploads/1784643950606-262721-SCR-20260721-owkp.png)

The two filters combine, so you can ask a precise question like "YouTube affiliates with at least 50,000 subscribers" in a few clicks.

### Sort by reach

To rank affiliates from biggest audience to smallest, select the **Social Channels** column header and sort it — the list orders by audience size so your highest-reach affiliates rise to the top. You can also show or hide columns from the columns menu if you want the Social Channels column always in view. For more on shaping the list, see [Filters and columns](/help-center/manage/affiliates/filters-and-columns).

![social-channels-column](https://qnthgdfusotvjgaxpfie.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/public-assets/docs/uploads/1784643957772-67953-SCR-20260721-oyie.png)

## Read an affiliate's audience data

Filtering tells you who has reach; the **Audience** tab tells you who that reach is. Open any affiliate from your list and select the **Audience** tab to see everything Referly has pulled from their connected accounts.

![audience-tab-overview](https://qnthgdfusotvjgaxpfie.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/public-assets/docs/uploads/1784643975725-230583-SCR-20260721-oyrq.png)

Each connected platform gets its own section. Depending on the platform, you'll see:

- **Headline stats** — followers or subscribers, total views, posts or videos, likes, and following, shown as cards at the top of the platform's section.
- **Audience demographics** — the age and gender split of the affiliate's audience, for platforms that share it (YouTube and Instagram). Switch between the **Gender** and **Age Group** views to see how their audience breaks down.
- **Geography** — a world map of the affiliate's top countries, so you can see whether their audience matches the markets you sell into. Instagram also surfaces top cities.
- **Traffic sources and devices** — for YouTube and a connected website, where the audience comes from (search, suggested, direct, social, and so on) and what devices they use.
- **Website metrics** — for a connected website, monthly visitors, sessions, pageviews, bounce rate, engagement rate, and average session length, pulled from the site's analytics.

![audience-demographics](https://qnthgdfusotvjgaxpfie.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/public-assets/docs/uploads/1784643987365-862038-SCR-20260721-pfxe.jpeg)

![audience-geography](https://qnthgdfusotvjgaxpfie.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/public-assets/docs/uploads/1784644001480-453858-SCR-20260721-ozrx.jpeg)

A couple of things to expect:

- **Insights need a verified account.** If an affiliate added a platform but hasn't verified it, that section shows a prompt instead of data. The demographics and geography appear once they verify ownership.
- **Small audiences may show less.** Platforms hold back demographic breakdowns for accounts below a certain size to protect their users' privacy, so a very small account may show follower counts but no age, gender, or country data. This is a platform rule, not a Referly limit.

Together, the filters and the Audience tab turn a list of names into a picture of real reach — so you can spot the affiliates worth a closer look, tailor your outreach to the ones whose audience fits, and prioritize where you spend your time.
