Name and email tell you almost nothing about a new affiliate. Custom questions let you ask what you actually need to know before you approve someone — which platforms they post on, how big their audience is, why they want to join — and let you collect files like an ID or a media kit.

Custom questions live inside your **onboarding**: the short guided flow every new affiliate goes through right after they sign up. You add a **custom step**, then add your questions inside it.

To find these settings, open **Settings** from the left sidebar, go to **Affiliate Portal**, then select **Onboarding**.

![onboarding-settings-page](https://qnthgdfusotvjgaxpfie.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/public-assets/docs/uploads/1784765348844-465174-SCR-20260723-doun.png)

<Note>
  Custom questions used to have their own settings page. They're now part of **Onboarding**, so everything is in one place. If you land on the old page, it points you here.
</Note>

<Info>
  Adding a **custom step** and its questions needs the **Business plan** or higher. You can rearrange and edit the built-in steps on any plan. See [plans and features](/help-center/billing/plans-and-features).
</Info>

## Turn onboarding on

At the top left of the page, **Enable onboarding** controls whether new affiliates see the flow at all. Your questions only get asked when this is on.

Two options sit next to it:

- **Allow skipping** lets affiliates skip the whole onboarding and go straight into their portal. Leave it off if your questions matter to you.
- **Show confetti** plays a small celebration when an affiliate finishes.

## Add a custom step

A custom step is the screen that holds your questions. In the **Steps** section, select **Add custom step**, and a new step appears in the list ready to set up.

![add-custom-step-button](https://qnthgdfusotvjgaxpfie.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/public-assets/docs/uploads/1784765543920-49876-SCR-20260723-dphg.jpeg)

Expand the new step and fill in these fields:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Name the step">
    **Title** is the heading the affiliate reads, and **Subtitle** is the line of helper text under it. For example, a title of "Tell us about your audience" with a subtitle of "This helps us support you better."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick an icon">
    Choose a **Step Icon** to represent the step in the flow.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Decide if it can be skipped">
    Turn on **Skippable** to let affiliates move past the step without answering. Leave it off to make the whole step mandatory.
  </Step>
</Steps>

![custom-step-fields](https://qnthgdfusotvjgaxpfie.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/public-assets/docs/uploads/1784765549319-898312-SCR-20260723-dpku.png)

You can add more than one custom step — for example, one for audience details and another for verification documents.

## Choose a question type

Inside your custom step, find the **Questions** area and select **Add question**. The **Choose question type** window opens with five options, and your choice decides what the affiliate sees and what kind of answer you get back.

![question-type-picker](https://qnthgdfusotvjgaxpfie.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/public-assets/docs/uploads/1784765593504-718861-SCR-20260723-dqoh.png)

| Type | Best for | What the affiliate sees |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Short Text** | Quick answers, like a handle or company name | A single-line text box |
| **Long Text** | Details in their own words, like why they want to join | A larger, multi-line text box |
| **Dropdown** | Many options to choose one from, like a country | A dropdown menu of your options |
| **Multiple Choice** | A shorter range of answers | Selectable options you define |
| **Image Upload** | Files, like an ID or a media kit | An upload box |

## Set up each question

Select a question to expand it. Every question has a **Label** — the question the affiliate actually reads, such as "Which platforms do you post on?"

![question-settings](https://qnthgdfusotvjgaxpfie.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/public-assets/docs/uploads/1784765565519-341013-SCR-20260723-dqas.png)

The rest of the settings depend on the type you picked:

- **Placeholder** (Short Text and Long Text) — faint example text inside the empty box to hint at what to type.
- **Options** (Dropdown and Multiple Choice) — the choices you offer. Select **Add option** for each one. For Multiple Choice, turn on **Multiple selections** to let affiliates pick more than one.
- **Multiple files** (Image Upload) — allow more than one file, up to four.

Two settings apply to every question, whatever the type:

- **Required** — the affiliate has to answer before they can continue. Leave it off to make it optional.
- **Editable** — the affiliate can change their answer later from their portal. Turn it off to lock the answer once it's given, which is useful for anything you've reviewed, like an uploaded ID.

## Reorder, hide, or remove steps

The **Steps** list is drag-and-drop. Drag a step by its grip handle to change where it falls in the flow, so your questions come exactly where you want them.

{/* TODO screenshot: reorder-steps — upload it in the local Documentation Agent */}

- The **Summary** step is always pinned last.
- Use the eye icon to **hide** a step. It keeps its settings but stops showing to affiliates, which is handy for pausing a question set without losing it. Built-in steps can only be hidden, not deleted.
- Use the trash icon to **delete** a custom step. Only custom steps can be deleted.
- Drag questions inside a step to reorder them the same way.

## Preview and save

The **Preview** panel shows your onboarding exactly as an affiliate would see it, and you can switch between a desktop and a mobile view to check both.

When you're happy, select **Save Changes**.

<Check>
  When the "Onboarding settings saved" message appears, your questions are live for new affiliates.
</Check>

## Where to read the answers

Every answer is saved to the affiliate's record. Open the affiliate from your [affiliates list](/help-center/manage/affiliates/overview) and go to the **Answers** tab on their detail page.

![answers-tab](https://qnthgdfusotvjgaxpfie.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/public-assets/docs/uploads/1784765570467-896483-SCR-20260723-dqgj.png)

Answers are grouped by the step they came from, so a step named "Tell us about your audience" appears as its own section. Text answers show as written, dropdown and multiple-choice answers show what was selected, and uploaded files appear as thumbnails you can open full size.

This is what you read before [approving or declining an application](/help-center/getting-started/recruit/approve-or-decline).

Affiliates can revisit their answers from **Settings** in their own portal, where each custom step appears under **Personal info**. They can change an answer only if you marked that question **Editable**.
