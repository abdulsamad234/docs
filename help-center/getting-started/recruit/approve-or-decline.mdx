By default, anyone who joins your program is approved automatically and can start promoting straight away. That's the fastest path to growth, but it isn't always what you want. If you'd rather vet people first — check who they are, read what they told you, and keep out anyone who isn't a fit — you can turn automatic approval off and review each application yourself before it goes live.

This page shows how to switch off automatic approval, how to apply that choice across your commission plans or set it plan by plan, what an affiliate sees while they wait, and how you approve or decline each application.

## Turn off automatic approval

Whether new affiliates go live on their own is controlled by your **Auto-approve settings**. These live on your base commission plan — the baseline terms that apply to your whole program.

To find them, go to **Settings**, open **Program details**, and select **General**. Find the **Auto-approve settings** card and open it.

![base-auto-approve-settings](https://qnthgdfusotvjgaxpfie.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/public-assets/docs/uploads/1784646158819-76871-SCR-20260721-pqlu.png)

Inside, turn off **Auto approve affiliates**. The helper text reads **New affiliates are approved immediately when enabled** — so switching it off does the opposite: new affiliates wait for you instead of going live on their own.

- **On** (the default): anyone who joins is approved immediately and can start promoting right away.
- **Off**: new affiliates land in a pending state and can't promote until you review and approve them.

The same card holds **Auto approve rewards**, which does the same thing for the commissions affiliates earn rather than for the affiliates themselves. This page is about approving affiliates — for reviewing rewards before they're paid, see [Approve or decline rewards](/help-center/manage/rewards/approve-or-decline).

Select **Save** when you're done. From now on, new affiliates joining on your base terms wait for your review.

## Apply the setting to every commission plan

Each [commission plan](/help-center/getting-started/commissions/overview) carries its own approval setting, so turning off automatic approval on your base plan doesn't automatically change the plans you've built with their own terms. The **Apply to all commission plans** toggle, in the same **Auto-approve settings** card, is how you make one choice stick everywhere.

![apply-to-all-plans](https://qnthgdfusotvjgaxpfie.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/public-assets/docs/uploads/1784646165920-326747-SCR-20260721-pqes.png)

When it's on, the approval choice you just made is pushed to **all current and future plans** — every custom plan you already have is updated to match, and any new plan you create starts out the same way. Its helper text reads **Applies to all current and future plans**. This is the setting to use when you want a single, consistent rule across your whole program: review everyone, or review no one.

When it's off, your base plan setting only affects affiliates on your base terms. Each custom plan keeps whatever it's set to on its own — which is what the next section covers.

## Set approval per commission plan

If you'd rather review affiliates on some plans but let others in automatically, leave **Apply to all commission plans** off and set each plan's approval independently.

Open the [commission plan](/help-center/getting-started/commissions/overview) you want to change and expand its **Auto-approve settings** panel. It has the same **Auto approve affiliates** toggle as your base plan, but it only affects affiliates who join on that plan.

![plan-auto-approve](https://qnthgdfusotvjgaxpfie.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/public-assets/docs/uploads/1784646172281-545976-SCR-20260721-psmy.jpeg)

This lets you mix approaches. For example, you might auto-approve affiliates who join through a trusted partner's plan, while holding everyone from your public sign-up page for review. Just remember: if you later turn **Apply to all commission plans** back on, it overwrites these per-plan choices with your base setting.

## What affiliates see while they wait

When approval is required, an affiliate finishes signing up as normal, but instead of landing in their portal they see a waiting screen. It reads **Wait while we review your affiliate application**, with the reassurance **We will let you know once we approve your account** and a **Log out** button.

![affiliate-pending-view](https://qnthgdfusotvjgaxpfie.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/public-assets/docs/uploads/1784646178692-996083-SCR-20260721-ptun.png)

They can't reach their referral link, assets, or dashboard until you approve them, so nothing is tracked or earned while they wait. Once you approve them, they get in and can start promoting. If you decline them, they see a message telling them their application was declined and to contact you if they think it's a mistake.

## Review and approve or decline an application

Affiliates awaiting your review show up in your [affiliates list](/help-center/manage/affiliates/overview) with a **Pending** status, so you can spot who's waiting. Select an affiliate to open their detail page, where you decide.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the pending affiliate">
    From your affiliates list, select the person you want to review to open their [detail page](/help-center/manage/affiliates/affiliate-detail).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review what they submitted">
    Read what you know about them before you decide. If you [ask application questions](/help-center/getting-started/recruit/application-questions), open the **Answers** tab to see everything they told you when they signed up — the platforms they promote on, their audience, or any documents you asked them to upload.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve or decline">
    At the top of the page you'll see two buttons: **Approve Affiliate** and **Reject Affiliate**. They only appear while an affiliate is pending.
  </Step>
</Steps>

![approve-decline-buttons](https://qnthgdfusotvjgaxpfie.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/public-assets/docs/uploads/1784646185995-318395-SCR-20260721-pual.png)

### Approve an affiliate

Select **Approve Affiliate**. A confirmation asks **Are you sure you want to approve this affiliate? They will be able to start promoting your campaign immediately.** Select **Approve** to confirm.

![approve-confirm-dialog](https://qnthgdfusotvjgaxpfie.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/public-assets/docs/uploads/1784646192744-997428-SCR-20260721-pugs.png)

The affiliate becomes active right away. Their waiting screen is replaced by their full portal, their referral link and any promo code start working, and their referrals and commissions are tracked from then on.

### Decline an affiliate

Select **Reject Affiliate** to turn the application down. A confirmation asks **Are you sure you want to reject this affiliate? This action cannot be undone.** Select **Reject** to confirm.

The affiliate's status changes to **Declined**, and they see a message letting them know their application wasn't accepted, with a note to contact you if they believe it's a mistake. As the confirmation warns, this can't be undone, so read their details before you decline.
