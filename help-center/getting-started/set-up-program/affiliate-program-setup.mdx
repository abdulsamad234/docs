When you sign in to Referly for the first time, a short setup wizard walks you through creating your program. By the end you have a program, an affiliate portal, and a commission structure, and once you choose a plan you land on your dashboard. It takes a couple of minutes.

This guide covers each step so you know what every field does and what to expect. It stops at the point where you connect a payment or ecommerce integration, which has its own guide, and points you there as the next step.

<Info>
  You need a Referly account to start. If you have not created one yet, [sign up here](https://referly.so/sign-up). If you sign up with your email address, Referly first sends you a confirmation link: select it to finish creating your account. New accounts open the onboarding wizard automatically at `/onboarding`.
</Info>

## What you set up during onboarding

The wizard has three steps and a final screen. A progress bar and a step counter (**Step 1 of 4**, **Step 2 of 4**, and so on) at the top show where you are:

1. **Tell us about your business**: your business type and category.
2. **Set up your referral program**: website, name, subdomain, and logo. Your program is created here.
3. **Set up your commission structure**: how affiliates are rewarded.
4. **You're all set**: copy your affiliate portal link, choose a plan to activate your program, and go to your dashboard.

From step 2 on, you can select **Back** to return to the previous step. You can change every choice later in [program settings](/help-center/settings/program/general).

## Step through the wizard

### Tell us about your business

Pick the business type that fits you best: **SaaS**, **E-commerce**, **Agency**, or **Other**. Once you choose a type, a row of category chips appears below it. Select the category that best describes your niche.

Referly uses these answers to tailor the rest of your setup. Both a type and a category are required before you can select **Continue**.

![Step 1 of 4 of the setup wizard, Tell us about your business, with E-commerce selected as the business type and Food and Beverage chosen as the category](/images/help-center/getting-started/onboarding-business-type-v2.png)

### Enter your program details

![Step 2 of 4 of the setup wizard, Set up your referral program, asking for your Website URL](/images/help-center/getting-started/onboarding-website-url.png)

This is where your program is created. Start by entering your **Website URL**, then select **Continue**.

Referly reads your website and pre-fills the rest of the form for you. If it can't read your site or find a logo, the fields still appear, so you can fill them in by hand:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Website URL">
    The address of the site you run your program for. It has to be a valid URL. Referly adds `https://` if you leave it off.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Program Name">
    Auto-suggested from your domain, for example `Acme Referral Program`. This is the name affiliates see. Edit it to anything you like.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Affiliate Portal Subdomain">
    The web address where affiliates log in, register, and track their performance and payouts, for example `acme.referly.so`. Referly suggests one and checks it for availability as you type. A green check means it is free; a red message means it is taken, so try another. You can add a [custom domain](/help-center/settings/portal/custom-domain) later.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Program Logo">
    Auto-fetched from your website. Select **Change** to upload your own image, or **Remove** to clear it. If no logo was found, select **Upload** to add one. Maximum file size is 1 MB, and a square image of 512 by 512 pixels or larger works best.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Entered the wrong website? Once the fields appear, select the pencil icon inside the **Website URL** field to edit it. Select the tick to save: Referly reads the new site and refills **Program Name**, **Affiliate Portal Subdomain**, and **Program Logo** to match, replacing anything you typed or uploaded there. If you've already created your program, it's updated too. Select the X instead to cancel the change.

When the details look right, select **Create Program**. Referly creates your program and the affiliate portal, then moves you to the commission step.

![Step 2 of the setup wizard with the Program Name, the Affiliate Portal Subdomain ending in .referly.so, the Program Logo and the Back, Skip and Continue buttons](/images/help-center/getting-started/onboarding-program-details-v2.png)

<Note>
  If you go back to this step later in the flow, Referly reuses the program you already created instead of making a duplicate.
</Note>

### Set your commission structure

Decide how and when you reward affiliates. Under **How do you want to reward your affiliates?**, choose a reward type:

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Cash rewards">
    Pay affiliates a monetary commission. Configure:

    - **When affiliates earn commission**: **Customer Makes a Payment** (pay when a referred customer buys) or **New Lead Signs Up** (pay a flat rate when a referred person creates an account).
    - **Commission frequency** (payment trigger only): **First Payment Only**, or **Recurring Basis** to pay on multiple customer payments. If you choose recurring, set a limit: until the customer cancels, by number of months, by number of payments, or both.
    - **Commission type and rate** (payment trigger only): **Percentage** (a share of each sale, for example 10%) or **Flat Rate** (a fixed amount per sale). Sign-up commissions are always a fixed amount.
    - **Currency**: shown next to a fixed amount. It defaults to USD.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Non-cash rewards">
    Reward affiliates with a **Coupon** or **Custom Credits** instead of cash. You choose the reward type, when it is earned, and its settings.

    - **Coupons** can be created through the **API**, or directly in **Shopify**, **Stripe**, **WooCommerce**, **Creem**, or **Ecwid**.
    - **Credits** can be your own custom unit, or store credits processed through Shopify, Stripe, WooCommerce, or Creem (**Creem Credits**).

    You can pick non-cash rewards here before you've chosen a plan. See [non-cash rewards](/help-center/getting-started/commissions/non-cash-rewards) for the full setup.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

Select **Continue** to save your commission settings to the program. You can refine them and reach advanced options anytime. See the [commissions overview](/help-center/getting-started/commissions/overview) to learn how commissions work.

![Step 3 of 4 of the setup wizard, Set up your commission structure, with Cash Rewards, Customer Makes a Payment, First Payment Only and Percentage selected and a 20 percent rate entered](/images/help-center/getting-started/onboarding-commission-structure-v2.png)

### Finish and land on your dashboard

The final screen, headed **Setup Complete**, confirms your program is created. A card under **Your affiliate program** shows your program's logo and name, plus your affiliate portal link. Select the copy icon to copy the link, or the open icon to visit your portal in a new tab.

What comes next depends on whether your account already has a plan:

- **No plan yet**: the card shows an **Inactive** badge, because your program isn't active until you choose a plan. Select **Activate now** to jump to the plans, shown under **Start your free trial** (or **Choose your plan** if a free trial isn't available to you). Pick a plan to activate your program. **View full pricing comparison** opens the full pricing page in a new tab. See [plans and features](/help-center/billing/plans-and-features) for what each plan includes.
- **Already on a plan**: the card reminds you to share the link with affiliates. Under **What's Next?** you'll see three suggestions: **Integrate Referly**, **Customize Affiliate Program**, and **Invite Affiliates**. Select **Go to Dashboard** to finish.

![Step 4 of 4, You're all set, showing the new affiliate program card with its portal address and Live badge, the What's Next suggestions and the Go to Dashboard button](/images/help-center/getting-started/onboarding-completion-v2.png)

<Note>
  Installed Referly from the Shopify App Store or the Ecwid App Market? The final screen reads **Your program is ready** and, in place of the plans, shows **Connect your Shopify store** or **Connect your Ecwid store**. Select it to link your store to your new program. With Shopify, you approve the permissions on Shopify, then come back to start your free trial. With Ecwid, your store is already authorized, so one click links it and then you pick your plan. **Skip for now** leaves the store connection for later.
</Note>

<Check>
  Your program, affiliate portal, and commission structure are set up. You are on the dashboard, ready to connect tracking and recruit affiliates.
</Check>

![Referly dashboard home after onboarding, with the revenue chart, Action Items and Quick Links](/images/help-center/getting-started/program-dashboard-v2.png)

The **Complete your setup** checklist in your dashboard's top bar shows what to do next. See [Your setup checklist](/help-center/getting-started/set-up-program/setup-checklist).

<Note>
  Creating another program later? When you select **Create new program** from the program switcher, or an agency creates a client program, a shorter version of these steps opens inside your dashboard. If the agency has added its own domain, a client program's subdomain and portal link use that domain instead of `referly.so`.
</Note>

## Next step: connect your tracking

Onboarding creates your program, but sales do not flow in until Referly is connected to where your payments or orders happen. Connecting a payment or ecommerce platform is the natural next step.
