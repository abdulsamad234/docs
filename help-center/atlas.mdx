Referly Atlas is the ad-tracking side of Referly. It records every visit to your store, matches each order or conversion to the visit that produced it, and sends the event to the ad platforms you use from Referly's servers. Browser pixels miss a share of conversions because of ad blockers, cookie limits and consent banners. Server-side events reach the platform anyway, so your ads optimise on more of your real sales.

Atlas works next to your affiliate program with the same Referly account and the same team. It has its own plan, its own pages in the left sidebar, and its own help section, which starts here.
