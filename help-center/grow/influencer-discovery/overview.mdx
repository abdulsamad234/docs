Influencer Discovery is where you find creators to recruit into your program. Instead of cold-emailing strangers and hoping they can sell, you browse a directory of people who are **already affiliates on Referly** — creators who have connected their social channels, promoted other programs, and in many cases driven real revenue. You can see how each one performs, how well they fit your program, and invite the ones you like to join yours.

Think of it as a warm talent pool. Everyone here has already opted into affiliate marketing and been active somewhere on the network, so you're starting from a much stronger position than a blank outreach list.

## What you can do here

Influencer Discovery gives you one place to run your whole recruiting workflow:

- **Browse and search** a network of vetted creators, filtered down to the ones that fit your niche, audience size, and revenue history.
- **Read a match score** on every creator — an estimate of how well they'd do in *your* program specifically.
- **Save** the ones worth a closer look into folders, so you can build a shortlist before you reach out.
- **Invite** creators to join your program, and track who accepted.

Each of those steps has its own page in this section — this article is the map.

## Where to find it

Open the **Grow** section in your dashboard sidebar and select **Influencer Discovery**. It opens on the **Discover** tab, with a toolbar for searching and filtering at the top and a grid of creator cards below.

## How the tabs are organized

The page is split into four tabs that follow a creator from first sighting to fully recruited. The number on each tab shows how many creators are in it.

![discovery-tabs](https://qnthgdfusotvjgaxpfie.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/public-assets/docs/uploads/1784761345088-214458-SCR-20260723-crdr.png)

- **Discover** — the full directory. This is where you search, filter, and find new creators.
- **Saved** — creators you've bookmarked, organized into folders. Your shortlist lives here.
- **Invited** — creators you've invited to your program, including any who declined.
- **Recruited** — creators who accepted your invitation and are now affiliates in your program.

The **Saved**, **Invited**, and **Recruited** tabs are your recruiting pipeline — see [Saved, invited, and recruited](/help-center/grow/influencer-discovery/pipeline) for how creators move through them.

## What each card tells you

Every creator in the directory is shown as a card, so you can compare people at a glance before opening anyone's full profile.

![influencer-card](https://qnthgdfusotvjgaxpfie.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/public-assets/docs/uploads/1784761350316-325631-SCR-20260723-crix.png)

On each card you'll see:

- **Their name and photo**, plus a country flag showing where they're based.
- **A status badge**, so you always know whether you've already saved, invited, or recruited this person.
- **A `% match` chip** in the top right — how well this creator fits your program. Select it to see the full breakdown of how that number is calculated. See [How the match score works](/help-center/grow/influencer-discovery/match-score).
- **Social channels** — chips for the platforms they've connected (such as YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, or X), with a check mark on verified accounts.
- **Categories** — the niches they create in and have earned in, so you can tell whether they're a fit for your product.
- **A stats footer** — headline numbers like total audience, revenue they've generated across programs, and their return on investment.

Select any card to open that creator's full profile, where you can dig into their audience, channel-by-channel stats, and estimated earnings before you decide to reach out.

## A typical workflow

Most programs use Influencer Discovery in the same rhythm:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Narrow the directory">
    On the **Discover** tab, search and filter down to creators in your niche, at the audience size and revenue level you're after. See [Search and filter](/help-center/grow/influencer-discovery/search).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the fit">
    Sort by **Best match** and open the creators that look strongest. Their [match score](/help-center/grow/influencer-discovery/match-score) tells you who's most likely to perform in your program.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build a shortlist">
    **Save** the promising ones into folders so you can review them as a group before reaching out. They'll show up on your **Saved** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite your picks">
    Send an invitation to the creators you want. See [Invite an influencer](/help-center/grow/influencer-discovery/invite).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Welcome the ones who join">
    Anyone who accepts moves to your **Recruited** tab and becomes an affiliate in your program, ready to start promoting.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Where the data comes from

The creators you see aren't scraped from the open web — they're real affiliates who joined programs on Referly and connected their own social accounts. Their audience and performance numbers are refreshed daily, and their match score is calculated live against your program's categories, customers, and revenue. That means the directory stays current, and the fit you're shown reflects your program as it looks today.
