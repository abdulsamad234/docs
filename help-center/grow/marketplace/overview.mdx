The Marketplace is a public directory of affiliate programs on Referly. Affiliates browse it to find products worth promoting — and when you publish a listing, your program shows up there for them to discover and join on their own.

It's the inbound side of recruiting. Where [Influencer Discovery](/help-center/grow/influencer-discovery/overview) is you reaching out to creators, the Marketplace is affiliates coming to you. A good listing works around the clock: affiliates find it, read your offer, and sign up without you lifting a finger.

## Where your listing appears

Once published, your listing can surface in several places affiliates look:

- The **Marketplace home**, including the featured programs carousel and category showcases.
- **Category and search results**, when affiliates filter to your niche or reward type.
- Inside **affiliate portals**, where existing affiliates browse for more programs to promote.

![marketplace-home](https://qnthgdfusotvjgaxpfie.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/public-assets/docs/uploads/1784762871140-270642-SCR-20260723-dagb.png)

How high you appear in these places is decided by your **visibility score** — a measure of how strong and complete your listing is. See [Visibility and boosts](/help-center/grow/marketplace/visibility).

## How affiliates find and join you

<Steps>
  <Step title="They browse or search">
    Affiliates explore the marketplace by category, reward, or search, and open listings that catch their eye.
  </Step>
  <Step title="They read your listing">
    Your listing page shows your branding, your pitch, the reward on offer, and answers to common questions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="They join">
    When an affiliate joins from your listing, they're placed straight into the affiliate group you chose for the listing — so they land on the right commission terms automatically and can start promoting.
  </Step>
</Steps>

That means every affiliate who comes through the marketplace arrives already assigned to a plan you control — you decide the terms once, when you build the listing.

## Manage your listing

Open the **Grow** section in your dashboard and select **Marketplace**. This is your listing editor, where you build the listing, preview it, and publish it. Your listing is either a **Draft** (only you can see it) or **Published** (live in the marketplace).

![my-listing-editor](https://qnthgdfusotvjgaxpfie.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/public-assets/docs/uploads/1784762877136-390739-SCR-20260723-daks.png)

<Note>
  Building and saving a listing as a draft is free. **Publishing** it to the live marketplace requires an active **marketplace license**. See [Marketplace licenses](/help-center/grow/marketplace/licenses).
</Note>
